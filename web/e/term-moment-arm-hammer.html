Moment arm (hammers/axes)

For a hammer or axe weapon, the distance from the pivot to the head's center of mass (and to its impact point). A longer moment arm gives the head a higher tip speed and more kinetic energy for the same swing torque and angular acceleration, but also increases the weapon's MOI (which scales with the square of that distance), demanding more torque/time to swing and putting more bending stress on the pivot and its bearings/shaft during the strike.
