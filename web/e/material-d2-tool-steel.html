D2 Tool Steel

High-carbon, high-chromium air-hardening tool steel capable of very high hardness (58-62 HRC) — used for hardened inserts, drill-resistant plates, and edge-holding weapon teeth where wear resistance matters more than shock toughness.

Strengths

  • Very high attainable hardness — excellent wear/abrasion resistance and edge holding
  • Cheaper than carbide while still very hard
  • Good as small drill-resistant inserts protecting critical internals

Weaknesses

  • Noticeably more brittle than S7 at equivalent hardness — more prone to chipping under shock
  • Not a good choice for a full weapon bar that takes repeated direct spinner hits

Notes

D2 sits between tough-but-softer S7/4140 and ultra-hard-but-ultra-brittle tungsten carbide: it's a reasonable choice for a small hardened plate protecting a battery or receiver against drill/screwdriver-style piercing weapons, but a poor choice for a primary weapon bar that needs to survive repeated hard impacts without chipping.
