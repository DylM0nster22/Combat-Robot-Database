Moment of inertia — point mass at a radius

MOI of a small, compact mass treated as concentrated at a single point, a distance r from the rotation axis. Used for bolts, counterweights, or small hard-faced tips whose own size is negligible next to their distance from the axis.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightrotationalmoiweapon-inertia

Expression

I = m * r^2

Variables

Imoment of inertia, kg*m^2
mmass of the point-like object, kg
rdistance from the rotation axis to the mass, m

Worked example

A small steel balance-counterweight bolt has mass m = 0.005 kg (5 g) mounted at radius r = 0.040 m. I = 0.005 0.040^2 = 0.005 0.0016 = 0.000008 kgm^2 (8.0e-6 kgm^2).
