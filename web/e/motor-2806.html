2806 brushless motor (weapon)

28mm x 6mm stator, entry point of the 28xx family; used on heavy 1 lb builds and as a light-beetleweight crossover weapon motor, usually belt-driven to keep tip speed in range at low KV.

Strengths

Weaknesses
