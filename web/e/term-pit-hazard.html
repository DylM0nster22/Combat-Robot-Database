Pit / Arena Hazard

A hole, drop, or automated hazard (spikes, hammers, saws) built into some arenas that can end a match instantly if a robot falls in or is forced into it — commonly touching the bottom of a pit is an automatic loss under UK/AWS-style rules. Larger UK 'house robot'-style arenas historically had multiple hazards; most hobby-class US arenas are simpler flat boxes without hazards.
