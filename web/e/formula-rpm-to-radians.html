RPM to angular velocity (rad/s) conversion

Simple unit conversion used throughout weapon-motor sizing math to move between RPM (what motor/ESC specs use) and rad/s (what physics formulas for torque, power and kinetic energy need).

Formulaformulaunit-conversion

Expression

omega_rads = RPM * pi / 30

Variables

omega_radsangular velocity, radians/second
RPMrevolutions per minute

Worked example

At 21,000 RPM: omega = 21000 pi / 30 = 21000 0.10472 = 2199 rad/s.
