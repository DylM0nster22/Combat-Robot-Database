FingerTech Lite Flite Foam Wheel

FingerTech's neoprene foam wheel, the most widely used antweight wheel: extremely light, shock-absorbing, and surprisingly grippy for a plain foam tire, sold in a wide range of diameters and two widths to fit the Twist/Snap/Lite hub system.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightwheelfoamfingertechlightweight

Strengths

  • Very light — a major reason it's the default antweight wheel choice
  • Neoprene foam absorbs shock from arena floor impacts and hazards, protecting the gearbox/shaft
  • Surprisingly good grip for its weight, especially versus hard plastic wheels
  • Wide diameter range (25.4-76.2mm / 1.0-3.0in in 6.35mm/0.25in steps) lets builders dial in speed vs torque without changing motors

Weaknesses

  • Less absolute grip than a silicone-coated or compliant rubber tread wheel
  • Foam compresses/wears over many hard hits, eventually needs replacement
  • Needs a separate hub (Lite/Snap/Twist) adding a small amount of weight and an assembly step

Notes

A 2.00in diameter tire alone weighs about 0.14oz (~4g); smaller/larger diameters scale roughly with tire volume. Widths available: 12.7mm (0.5in, "Thin") and 19.1mm (0.75in, "Wide"). Plastic hubs used with Lite Flites weigh about 3g each, so a full wheel+hub assembly at 1.5-2in is roughly 7-10g depending on width and hub choice.
