Walker

Legged locomotion instead of wheels, which most rulesets reward with a large weight bonus.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightwalkerbonusadvanced

Strengths

  • A true walker bonus can double the weight limit, which is an enormous advantage
  • Immune to having its wheels attacked
  • Novel and popular with crowds and judges

Weaknesses

  • Walking mechanisms are slow, complex and fragile
  • Qualifying as a 'true walker' is strictly judged — no wheels or cams touching the ground
  • The mechanism eats much of the bonus it earns

Notes

Weight bonuses for genuine legged locomotion are substantial — commonly up to double the class limit for a true walker, with a smaller bonus for shufflers. The catch is that qualifying is strict and the mechanism is heavy, so the bonus is less free than it looks. Confirm the exact bonus with your event before designing around it.
