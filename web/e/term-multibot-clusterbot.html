Multibot / Clusterbot

A single competition entry made of two or more physically separate robots controlled together as one 'weapon system,' sharing the weight limit of their class. Rules typically require all sub-units to be destroyed/immobilized for the team to lose, and extra weight allowance can apply if all sub-units are walkers.

Notes

Example from SPARC-style rules: a 12lb class multibot made of one walker/one shuffler is allowed 18lb total; a 12lb multibot made of two full walkers is allowed 24lb total. AWS (UK) clusterbots mixing roller and non-roller units use the formula: Roller allowance = Roller class limit - (2/3 x total non-roller weight), which prevents teams from gaming the non-roller bonus by loading most of the mass into non-rolling parts.
