Micro Combo RX + Dual Brushed ESC Board

An all-in-one micro PCB combining a radio receiver with two small brushed motor drivers, the standard drive-electronics solution in antweights because it saves the weight and wiring of separate RX and ESC boards.

Componentplastic-antweightelectronicsdrivecombo-boardbeginner

Strengths

  • One part instead of three (receiver + two ESCs), saving weight and wiring
  • Common failsafe-on-signal-loss behavior built in, satisfying rules requiring drive failsafe

Weaknesses

  • If it fails, the whole robot loses both drive and radio at once
  • Current rating (often 2-4A per channel) can limit motor choice
