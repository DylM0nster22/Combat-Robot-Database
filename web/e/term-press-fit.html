Press Fit

Holding a bearing or bushing by interference rather than adhesive or fasteners.

Termantweightplastic-antweightfabrication

Notes

A bearing seat should be a light interference fit — the bearing goes in with hand pressure or a light tap, not hammered. In a printed part, design the hole a few hundredths undersize and expect to tune it for your printer, because elephant's foot and shrinkage vary. A bearing that spins in its seat destroys the seat; a bearing pressed too hard binds and runs hot.
