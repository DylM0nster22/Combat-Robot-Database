COTS (Commercial Off-The-Shelf)

Parts bought ready-made rather than fabricated by the builder — motors, ESCs, receivers, bearings, and (per most plastic-ant FAQs) wheels/tires are explicitly allowed as COTS even though they aren't plastic, since the rule targets structural/armor/weapon plastic, not every component.
