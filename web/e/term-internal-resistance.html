Internal Resistance

The effective resistance inside a battery pack (cell chemistry plus connections) that causes its terminal voltage to drop under load; internal resistance rises with cell age, cycle count, cold temperature and physical damage, which is why an older or damaged pack sags far more than a fresh one of the same rated C rating.
