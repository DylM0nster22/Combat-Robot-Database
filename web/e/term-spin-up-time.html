Spin-Up Time

How long a weapon takes to reach its operating speed from rest (or from a partial-speed dip after a hit), set by the available average motor torque and the weapon's total moment of inertia. A liability when too long, since the weapon is weak while re-spinning.

Termantweightplastic-antweightspinnerphysicsstrategyrotationalspin-up

Notes

Spin-up time scales with weapon inertia and inversely with motor torque. A weapon that takes more than about three seconds is a liability: opponents will box-rush you at the start of the match and attack during every recovery. Reducing weapon mass or moving mass inward shortens spin-up at the cost of stored energy.
