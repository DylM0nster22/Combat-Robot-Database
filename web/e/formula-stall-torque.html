Stall torque from Kt and stall current

The maximum torque a motor can produce, occurring at zero RPM (locked rotor) when the full stall current flows.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightelectricaltorquestall

Expression

T_stall = Kt * I_stall

Variables

T_stallstall torque, N*m
Kttorque constant, N*m/A (use the exact 9.5493/Kv form)
I_stallstall current, A

Worked example

Kt (exact) = 0.003472 Nm/A, I_stall = 30 A (typical small antweight weapon brushless motor at 3S). T_stall = 0.003472 30 = 0.1042 Nm (about 1.06 kgfcm).
