Titanium Grade 2 (CP Titanium)

Commercially pure (unalloyed) titanium. Much more ductile and easier to form/weld than Grade 5, but roughly a third the yield strength — used where formability and fatigue resistance matter more than raw armor hardness.

Materialantweightplastic-antweighttitaniumarmorchassis

Strengths

  • Much more ductile/formable than Grade 5 — bends without cracking
  • Excellent corrosion resistance
  • Cheaper than Grade 5 per kg

Weaknesses

  • Roughly 1/3 the yield strength of Grade 5 — a poor choice for primary spinner armor
  • Still pricier than aluminum or steel for the strength delivered

Notes

Rarely the first choice for outer armor in antweights since Grade 5 gives so much more strength for similar weight, but Grade 2's ductility makes it useful for hinges, bent skirts, and springy standoffs that need to flex repeatedly without fatigue-cracking the way a harder alloy would.
