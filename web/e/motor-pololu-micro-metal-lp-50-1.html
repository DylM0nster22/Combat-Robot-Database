Pololu Micro Metal Gearmotor LP 6V, 50:1

The Low-Power (LP) tune of Pololu's micro metal gearmotor, drawing the least current of the three power tunes for a given ratio — useful when a builder is current-budget constrained (e.g. a very small ESC or shared receiver battery).

Componentantweightplastic-antweightdrive-motorbrushedpololumicro-metal-gearmotor

Strengths

  • Lowest current draw of the family, easiest on small ESCs
  • Same durable metal gearbox as MP/HP

Weaknesses

  • Least torque/power of the three tunes — can be traction- and power-limited on a heavier antweight
