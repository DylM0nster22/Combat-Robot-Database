Delrin (Acetal / POM)

Semi-crystalline engineering plastic (polyoxymethylene) known for low friction, good dimensional stability, and fatigue resistance — used for gears, bushings, and structural brackets rather than armor.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightlow-frictiongearsbushingsstructural

Strengths

  • Low coefficient of friction — great for sliding/rotating parts without lubrication
  • Good fatigue and creep resistance
  • Machines to tight tolerances easily
  • Decent impact toughness

Weaknesses

  • Not a great armor plastic — machines nicely but isn't as impact-tough as UHMW or polycarbonate
  • Poor UV resistance long-term
  • Hard to bond (low surface energy, similar issue to UHMW/HDPE)

Notes

Delrin's niche in combat robots is precision mechanical parts (gears, bushings, cam followers, servo linkages) rather than armor — its dimensional stability and low friction outweigh its middling impact toughness for those uses.
