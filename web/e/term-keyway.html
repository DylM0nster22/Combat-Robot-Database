Keyway

A machined slot cut lengthwise into both a shaft and its mating hub, into which a separate key (a small steel bar) is fitted so the two parts rotate together positively instead of relying on friction. Transmits high torque and survives shock loads far better than a set screw, but the slot itself is a stress concentration that can become the shaft's weak point, and cutting a clean small keyway in an ant-scale shaft (often 4-8 mm diameter) requires more precise machining than a set-screw flat or a broached hex.
