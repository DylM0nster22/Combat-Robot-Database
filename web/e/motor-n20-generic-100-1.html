Generic N20 Gearmotor, 6V, 100:1

A widely sold 12mm-diameter micro DC gearmotor (compatible with Sanyo NA4S-style gearboxes) used by budget and plastic-antweight builders as a cheap alternative to FingerTech/Repeat/Pololu motors, at the cost of unit-to-unit quality consistency.

Strengths

  • Extremely cheap (a few dollars each), widely available from many sellers
  • Small and light, good for plastic antweights on a tight budget

Weaknesses

  • Quality varies drastically unit to unit and listing to listing (the "quality lottery")
  • Many units mix plastic gears into otherwise "metal gear" listings
  • No standardized spec sheet across sellers — published numbers are unreliable

Notes

Base N20 motor is commonly quoted at ~15,000 RPM no-load at 6V for the bare motor before gearing; a 100:1 gearbox gives ~150 RPM output, consistent with a 298:1 listing observed selling at 50 RPM (15,000/298 ~= 50). Gearbox torque ratings published by manufacturers cap around 20 kg*mm (2.0 kg-cm) of continuous output torque for most ratios below 380:1, meaning the motor's theoretical stall torque at high ratios exceeds what the gearbox can survive — a mechanical, not electrical, limit. Price and exact weight vary a lot by seller; treat as representative, low-confidence figures.
