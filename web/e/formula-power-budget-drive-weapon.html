Total System Current Draw (Drive + Weapon)

Builds a rough total current budget for an antweight by summing estimated drive-motor stall/running current and weapon-motor running current, the starting point for both C-rating and capacity sizing.

Formulapower-budgetcurrent-drawsizing

Expression

I_total = (2 * I_drive_per_motor) + I_weapon_running

Variables

I_totalestimated combined system current, amps
I_drive_per_motorcurrent draw of one drive motor under typical pushing load (small antweight brushed gearmotors: roughly 1-3A typical, up to 5-8A stalled)
I_weapon_runningbrushless weapon motor current once at speed under normal cutting/spinning load (highly variable: roughly 3-10A typical for antweight-class brushless outrunners, spiking much higher at spin-up or when the weapon bites something)

Worked example

Two small brushed drive motors at ~2A each typical running current, plus a brushless weapon motor drawing ~8A running: I_total = (2*2) + 8 = 12A typical combined draw, with momentary spikes during weapon spin-up and simultaneous hard driving easily reaching 20-25A+ for a second or two — which is the spike the pack's burst rating (not its continuous rating) needs to absorb without collapsing.
