Outrunner vs inrunner

Outrunner motors spin the outer magnet-lined bell/can around a fixed inner stator (the common FPV/drone layout); inrunner motors spin a small magnet rotor inside a fixed outer stator can. Outrunners dominate weapon-motor use because their larger effective rotor radius gives more torque per amp and the bell itself doubles as a flywheel/weapon-mounting surface.

Notes

Inrunners (common in RC car/boat brushless motors) spin at much higher RPM for a given KV-equivalent and need external gearing to be useful as low-speed, high-torque weapon drives; they are rarely chosen for antweight weapons because outrunner FPV motors are cheaper, lighter, and already torque-biased.
