Monolithic weapon construction

A weapon machined or cut from a single piece of material with no separate tooth inserts -- the whole disc, bar, or drum is the same hardness throughout. Simpler and lighter (no fasteners or pockets), but a chip or crack in the edge is a chip or crack in the entire weapon, and the whole piece must be through-hardened or case-hardened as one part.
