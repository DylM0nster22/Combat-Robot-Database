National Havoc Robot League (NHRL)

A major US combat robot league best known for its livestreamed 3lb (beetleweight) and 12/30lb events; NHRL itself is not the primary home of the 1lb plastic ant class (its own plastic-material class is Plastic Beetleweight), but many smaller 'NHRL-adjacent' local and remote-competition circuits run 1lb plastic ant undercards using a similar remote-competition/streamed format.
