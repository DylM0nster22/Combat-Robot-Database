Repeat Robotics 2822 Weapon Motor

Purpose-built antweight weapon outrunner on a 28mm-diameter, 22mm-length can, sold specifically for direct-drive antweight spinners rather than repurposed from a drone; widely regarded as one of the most durable off-the-shelf antweight weapon motors.

Componentantweightweapon-motorpurpose-builtdirect-driveoutrunnerbread-and-butter

Strengths

  • Designed from the start for weapon shock loads, not adapted from a camera-drone motor
  • Low-ish KV (1100kv seen in the product line) suits direct drive at 4S without gearing down
  • Screw-retained bell rather than relying only on a C-clip, addressing the classic bell-separation failure

Weaknesses

  • Costs more than a generic same-size drone motor
  • Weight/price not independently confirmed for every winding in this listing
