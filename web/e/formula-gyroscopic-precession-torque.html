Gyroscopic precession torque from turning while the weapon spins

When a chassis carrying a spinning weapon is itself rotated (yawed) by the drivetrain, gyroscopic precession produces a reaction torque trying to tip the chassis about a THIRD axis, perpendicular to both the weapon's spin axis and the turn axis. This is the physics behind vertical-spinner 'gyro dance'.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightgyroscopicprecessiondrivetrain

Expression

tau_precession = I * omega_weapon * omega_turn

Variables

tau_precessionprecession (tipping) reaction torque, N*m
Iweapon moment of inertia about its spin axis, kg*m^2
omega_weaponweapon angular velocity, rad/s
omega_turnchassis turning (yaw) angular velocity commanded by the drivetrain, rad/s

Worked example

I = 3.25e-5 kgm^2, weapon at omega_weapon = 2094.4 rad/s (20,000 rpm), driver spins the chassis in place at omega_turn = 3.0 rad/s (~0.48 rev/s, a brisk turn-in-place). tau_precession = 3.25e-5 2094.4 3.0 = 3.25e-5 6283.2 = 0.2042 N*m.
