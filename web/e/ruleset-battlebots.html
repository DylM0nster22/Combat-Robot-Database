BattleBots Design & Safety Rules

BattleBots is the flagship US televised combat robotics league, running one professional heavyweight division at 250 lb ready-to-fight (up to 500 lb for a qualifying articulated-leg walker). Lifting/flipping weapons must pass a functional test lifting 250 lb to 12 inches before being allowed to compete, and every robot goes through a rigorous in-person tech and safety inspection ('the box') before being cleared to fight.

Notes

Judging point split (5 Damage / 3 Aggression / 3 Control on an 11-point scale) reflects the Judges' Guide used in recent seasons (Rev. 2021.0/2022.0); BattleBots has revised its judging system across seasons (e.g. the 2026 Pro League merges Aggression and Engagement), so builders/analysts should check the current-season Judges' Guide PDF rather than assume this split is permanent.
