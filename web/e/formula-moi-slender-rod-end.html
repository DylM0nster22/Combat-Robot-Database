Moment of inertia — slender rod about one end (hammer / axe / flail arm)

MOI of a uniform straight arm rotating about a pivot at one of its own ends, rather than its center — the correct model for a hammer, axe, or single-pivot flail arm.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightrotationalmoiweapon-inertia

Expression

I = (1/3) * m * L^2

Variables

Imoment of inertia, kg*m^2
mmass of the arm, kg
Llength of the arm from the pivot to its free tip, m

Worked example

A hammer weapon's swinging arm has mass m = 0.050 kg (50 g) and length L = 0.080 m (80 mm) from the pivot to the striking head. I = (1/3) 0.050 0.080^2 = (1/3) 0.050 0.0064 = (1/3) 0.00032 = 0.0001067 kgm^2 (1.067e-4 kg*m^2).
