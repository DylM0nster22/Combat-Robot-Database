Aluminum 7075-T6

High-strength aerospace aluminum alloy (zinc as primary alloying element), the strongest common aluminum grade — used for weapon hubs, structural frame rails, and lightweight brackets where steel is overkill.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightaluminumchassisstructureweapon-hub

Strengths

  • Best strength-to-weight of common aluminums
  • Machines beautifully
  • Widely available
  • Cheap relative to titanium

Weaknesses

  • Not weldable by common hobby methods (poor weldability vs 6061)
  • Lower corrosion resistance than 6061 — needs anodizing for long-term protection
  • Still far weaker than steel or titanium as outer spinner armor — dents/cracks under a hard hit

Notes

7075-T6 is a structural workhorse, not usually an outer-armor material in antweights — it is the go-to for weapon hubs, motor mount plates, and internal frame rails where you want steel-like strength at aluminum weight. Some plastic antweights use thin 7075 as a stiffening frame skeleton under 3D-printed shells.
