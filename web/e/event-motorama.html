Motorama

One of the longest-running US combat robotics events, historically held in Pennsylvania and now associated with Doswell, Virginia. Runs SPARC-aligned weight classes with a strong antweight/beetleweight/plastic-antweight community presence, and is a well-known season-opener on the US hobby calendar.
