SPARC (Standardized Procedures for the Advancement of Robotic Combat)

SPARC is the dominant unified US/international rule set for hobby-scale combat robotics. A single 'SPARC-legal' build is accepted at essentially every SPARC-compliant event worldwide, covering weight classes from fairyweight (150g) up to heavyweight (220 lb), plastic-class material rules, weight bonuses for walkers/shufflers, multibot/clusterbot rules, and standardized safety requirements.
