COTS Foam/Silicone Antweight Wheel

An off-the-shelf foam or silicone-tired wheel (e.g. FingerTech, Repeat Robotics, Pololu) explicitly allowed as a non-plastic-restricted exemption for wheels/tires under most plastic-ant rulesets, offering better and more consistent grip than a printed tire.
