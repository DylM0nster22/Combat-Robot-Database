Radio Fail-Safe

A required behavior of the receiver/ESC system: if the transmitter signal is lost (turned off, out of range, or interfered with), all motion (drive and weapon) must stop automatically rather than continue at the last commanded value. Nearly universal requirement (SPARC, NHRL, RoboGames, AWS) and is actively tested by inspectors turning off the transmitter during check-in.
