Modular Construction

Building the robot as separable subassemblies so damage is repaired by swapping a module.

Termantweightplastic-antweightfabrication

Notes

The design target is a ten-minute pit repair. That means: armour panels that unbolt from outside, a weapon assembly that comes off as a unit, drive pods that can be replaced without dismantling the chassis, and a wiring harness with connectors rather than solder joints at every module boundary. Robots built as one welded-together whole lose events to damage that a modular robot fixes between matches.
