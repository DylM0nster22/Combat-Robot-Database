Electric Flipper

A flipper driven directly by a high-torque motor or a released flywheel rather than a gas or spring store.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcontrolflipper

Strengths

  • Repeatable — no gas to run out of and no re-cock cycle in the flywheel variant
  • Simpler plumbing than pneumatics and legal under every ruleset
  • Doubles as a self-righting mechanism

Weaknesses

  • Much less instantaneous power than a spring or gas system
  • Draws heavily on the same battery as the drive
  • Gearbox takes the full shock of every flip

Notes

Electric flippers trade peak power for repeatability. At 1 lb the usual approach is a motor with a very large reduction driving a short, stiff arm — enough to upend an opponent even if it will not throw them across the arena.
