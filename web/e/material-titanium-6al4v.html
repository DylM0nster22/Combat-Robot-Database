Titanium 6Al-4V (lightweight high-strength tips/bars)

A titanium alloy offering roughly steel-level strength at about 60% of the density, used in weight-critical antweight spinner tips, bars, or hubs where every gram saved can be redirected into more weapon mass at the rim or more battery/drivetrain budget.
