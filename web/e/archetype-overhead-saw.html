Overhead Saw

A spinning blade on a pivoting arm that comes down onto the top of an opponent.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightspinnerimpactnovelty

Strengths

  • Attacks the top surface, which is usually the thinnest armour
  • Visually dramatic and scores well on aggression
  • Can be used to hold an opponent down

Weaknesses

  • Needs both a weapon motor and an arm actuator inside 454 g
  • Very little energy actually transfers — saws cut, and combat armour resists cutting
  • The arm is a structural weak point

Notes

Saws look spectacular and rarely win. In 1 lb the weight spent on an arm mechanism plus a blade motor buys less damage than the same grams in a disc. They persist because they are fun and because a saw that pins an opponent scores control points.
