Forkbot

A control robot with thin hinged forks that ride on the floor, sliding under an opponent before they can get under you.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcontrolpassivebudget

Strengths

  • Hinged forks self-level to the floor, beating fixed wedges on uneven arenas
  • Cheap, light and easy to replace between matches
  • Forks can be made sacrificial so damage costs nothing

Weaknesses

  • Forks bend, and a bent fork is worse than no fork
  • No damage output at all
  • Can be defeated by an opponent with even lower clearance

Notes

Forks beat fixed wedges because they float. A rigid wedge is only as good as the flattest part of the arena; hinged forks follow the floor and stay in contact. Make them from spring steel or hardened stock and expect to straighten them between matches.
