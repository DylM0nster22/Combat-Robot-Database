FingerTech "Gold Spark" 16mm Gearmotor

FingerTech's budget-tier 16mm gearmotor: the same Mabuchi FK-050 base motor as the Silver Spark but with a plastic (not all-metal) gear stage, sold in 8 ratios from 20:1 to 360:1. Positioned for hobby/mini-sumo/line-following use and lighter-duty antweight builds rather than front-line competitive drive.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightdrive-motorbrushed16mmfingertechbudget

Strengths

  • Cheaper than Silver Spark for the same base motor
  • Fine for plastic antweights and lighter-contact builds
  • Same 11mm face-mount pattern as Silver Spark for easy swapping

Weaknesses

  • Plastic final gear stage is explicitly rated to fail above 6.84 kg-cm (95 oz-in) output torque
  • Not recommended as the sole drive on a bot expected to take direct hits or heavy ramming loads
