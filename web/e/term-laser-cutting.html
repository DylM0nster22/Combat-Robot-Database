Laser Cutting

Laser cutting of sheet metal and some plastics.

Termantweightplastic-antweightfabrication

Notes

Fibre lasers cut steel and (with care) titanium well. CO2 lasers cut acrylic and wood cleanly. Neither cuts polycarbonate or UHMW acceptably — PC yellows and chars at the edge, and UHMW melts and re-welds. If your design depends on PC or UHMW, plan on routing, milling or sawing instead.
