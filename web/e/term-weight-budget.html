Weight Budget

The practice of planning, before printing or buying anything, how the full 454g allowance will be split between armor/structure, weapon, drive, electronics and fasteners — critical in plastic ants because printed structure typically consumes over half the total weight, far more than in a metal antweight of the same class.

Notes

At antweight the limit is 454 g and every gram is contested. A typical competitive vertical spinner runs roughly 30-35% weapon, 18-22% drive, 22-26% armour and chassis, 10-14% electronics and 7-9% battery. Control robots move the weapon share into drive and armour. Always leave 3-5% slack — robots come in heavier than the CAD says.
