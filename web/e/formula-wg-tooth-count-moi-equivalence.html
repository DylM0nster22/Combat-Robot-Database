Tooth count does not change stored energy

Splitting a fixed tooth mass at a fixed radius into more teeth does not change the weapon's moment of inertia or stored kinetic energy -- tooth count only changes bite depth and hit frequency, which is a commonly confused point.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightmoitooth-countbitemyth-busting

Expression

I = SUM(m_i * r_i^2) for point masses at the same radius; total I is unchanged if total mass and radius are held constant regardless of how many pieces the mass is divided into

Variables

m_imass of an individual tooth, kg
r_iradius of that tooth's center of mass, m

Worked example

20 g of tooth material at 30 mm radius. As one tooth: I = 0.0200.030^2 = 1.80e-5 kgm^2. As two 10 g teeth at the same 30 mm radius: I = 2(0.0100.030^2) = 29.0e-6 = 1.80e-5 kgm^2 -- identical. The stored energy and tip speed are the same either way; what changes is hit frequency (doubles) and bite depth per hit (halves, see the bite-depth formulas). Builders sometimes assume more teeth means a 'weaker hit' -- it does mean shallower bite per hit, but not less total energy delivered over several hits.
