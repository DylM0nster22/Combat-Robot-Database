Perimeters vs Infill

For combat parts, wall count matters far more than infill percentage.

Termantweightplastic-antweightprinting

Specifications

recommended perimeters5
recommended infill pct25

Notes

A printed part's stiffness and impact resistance come mostly from its outer shell. Going from 2 to 5 perimeters does more for a combat part than going from 20% to 60% infill, and costs less weight and far less print time. A practical antweight recipe: 4-6 perimeters, 4-6 top and bottom layers, and 20-30% infill. Past that you are adding weight where it does the least good.
