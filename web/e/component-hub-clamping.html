Clamping (split/pinch) hub

A hub with a slit and a clamping screw that squeezes the full bore diameter down onto the shaft, rather than a set screw biting a single point. Grips more of the shaft's circumference, avoids denting/deforming the shaft the way a point-contact set screw can, and holds up somewhat better under shock -- still a friction joint, so still weaker than a keyway or hex broach under hard impacts.

Componentantweightplastic-antweighthubhardwareattachment

Strengths

  • grips more shaft surface area than a point set screw, better shock resistance
  • does not dent/deform the shaft surface the way a set screw can

Weaknesses

  • heavier and slightly bulkier than a simple set-screw hub
  • still a friction joint -- can still slip under a severe enough impact
