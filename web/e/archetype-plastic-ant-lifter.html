Plastic Ant Lifter

A wedge-fronted robot with a servo- or small-motor-actuated arm that scoops and lifts an opponent to expose their underside or throw them, using printed linkages instead of the pneumatics common in bigger lifters.

Strengths

  • Can win by control and occasional big flips without needing a fast-spinning weapon
  • Lift arm doubles as self-righting mechanism

Weaknesses

  • Printed linkage pins/hinges wear and can strip under repeated combat loads
  • Slower to act than a spinner, needs good positioning
