Undercutter

A horizontal bar sweeping very low to the floor, striking beneath an opponent's armour line at wheels and ground clearance.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightspinnerhorizontallowcounter-meta

Strengths

  • Defeats the wedge that beats an ordinary horizontal — it goes under the wedge's edge
  • Attacks wheels and drive pods, which are the least armoured parts of most robots
  • Very hard for a low flat robot to present a safe face to

Weaknesses

  • Extremely sensitive to floor contact; a few millimetres of ride-height error destroys the weapon
  • Arena floor seams and dents are a real hazard
  • Difficult to build with adequate ground clearance in 1 lb

Notes

The undercutter is the specific counter to the wedge-heavy antweight meta. Because it runs below the wedge's leading edge, the wedge's shape no longer protects it. The cost is precision: the weapon must run a few millimetres off the floor and stay there after impacts.
