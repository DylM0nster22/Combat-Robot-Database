ABS

A strong, impact-resistant, comparatively low-density filament, one of the five universally-legal plastic-ant materials; harder to print reliably (needs an enclosure to prevent warping) but rewards the effort with a lighter, tougher part than PLA.

Materialplastic-antweightfilamentchassis-materiallegal-everywhereadvanced-printing

Strengths

  • Lowest density of the commonly-used legal plastics, saving weight for the same part size
  • Very impact resistant when printed well
  • Acetone-smoothable, which can weld layer lines together for extra strength

Weaknesses

  • Warps badly without an enclosure and a heated bed
  • Fumes require ventilation
  • Less beginner-friendly than PLA/PETG
