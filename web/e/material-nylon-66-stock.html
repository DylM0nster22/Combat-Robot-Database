Nylon 6/6 (Stock Sheet/Rod)

Tough, abrasion-resistant engineering plastic, hygroscopic (absorbs moisture which affects properties) — used for gears, wear pads, and some structural brackets in stock sheet/rod form.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightnylongearswear-resistant

Strengths

  • Very good abrasion/wear resistance
  • Tough — good impact resistance
  • Decently priced

Weaknesses

  • Hygroscopic — absorbs water from air, which changes dimensions and softens the part over time
  • Properties vary noticeably between dry-as-molded and moisture-equilibrated states

Notes

Stock nylon sheet/rod is mostly used for wear components (skid pads, gears, low-friction bushings) in antweights rather than armor; 3D-printed nylon filament (see PA6/PA12 entry) is more commonly discussed for printed parts.
