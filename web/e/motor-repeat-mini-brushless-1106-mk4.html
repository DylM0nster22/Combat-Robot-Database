Repeat Robotics "Mini" Brushless 1106 Mk4.1 Gearmotor

Repeat Robotics' flagship antweight brushless drive gearmotor: a custom-wound 1106 outrunner brushless motor built directly into a 2-stage, 28.5:1, all-metal 16mm planetary gearbox with a 4mm hardened output shaft, aimed at builders who want more speed and efficiency than brushed Silver Sparks at a similar weight.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightdrive-motorbrushlessplanetary16mmrepeat-roboticsoutrunner

Strengths

  • Brushless motor = no brush wear, higher efficiency, higher power density than a same-size brushed motor
  • Metal planetary gearbox handles shock loads better than spur gears
  • Rated for 2S-4S, so voltage alone can retune top speed without swapping hardware

Weaknesses

  • Requires a reversing brushless ESC (not a simple brushed H-bridge)
  • Sensorless brushless motors can cog/stutter at very low duty cycle / near-stall, an issue brushed motors don't have
  • Historically has had periods of being sold out due to demand

Notes

The earlier Mk3/Mk4 generation used a 1104-size custom-wound motor (~3500kv); Mk4.1 moved to a 1106 stator (more stator volume/copper) for more torque and power in the same 16mm gearbox footprint. Using a 3500kv assumption and the 28.5:1 gearbox: at 2S (7.4V) no-load motor speed is ~25,900 rpm giving ~909 rpm at the output shaft; at 3S (11.1V) ~1,363 rpm output; at 4S (14.8V) ~1,818 rpm output (all idealized, no-load, medium/low confidence since kv was reported for the earlier 1104 version, not confirmed for 1106).
