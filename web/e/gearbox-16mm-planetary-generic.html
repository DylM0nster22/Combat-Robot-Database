16mm Planetary Gearbox Module (generic/Repeat-style)

A multi-stage (typically 2-3 stage) all-metal planetary gearbox module in the same 16mm form factor popularized by FingerTech's spur-gear Spark line, used by Repeat Robotics and others to mate to brushed or brushless motors for higher torque density and durability than a comparable spur gearbox.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightgearboxplanetary16mmhigh-torque

Strengths

  • Higher mechanical efficiency (typically ~80-90%) than a comparable spur gearbox (~65-80%)
  • Planetary gear meshing distributes load across multiple planet gears, more shock-tolerant than a single spur gear train
  • More torque-dense for the same envelope size

Weaknesses

  • More expensive and complex to manufacture than a simple spur gearbox
  • Harder to hand-repair/re-lubricate in the field than an open spur gearbox
