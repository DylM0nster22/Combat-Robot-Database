S7 Tool Steel

A shock-resistant air/oil-hardening tool steel commonly used for antweight spinner tooth inserts and small striking edges, chosen because it can be hardened well into the 50s HRC while retaining good toughness against repeated impact (as opposed to a harder but more brittle tool steel).

Strengths

  • Best-in-class impact toughness for its hardness among tool steels — resists chipping/chunking under repeated hits
  • Air-hardening: less quench distortion/cracking risk than oil/water-hardening steels
  • Wide usable hardness window (48-58 HRC) lets builders tune toughness vs edge retention

Weaknesses

  • Pricier per kg than 4140/4340
  • Needs proper heat treat oven/process — hard to DIY well without one
  • At the top of its hardness range it does still eventually chip against equally hard opponents

Notes

Combat robot weapon bars and discs typically target 54-58 HRC on S7 — hard enough to bite into opposing armor and resist deformation, but still within S7's tough range so it survives spinner-vs-spinner clashes better than a harder, more brittle steel like D2 or hardened drill rod would. Many antweight vertical spinner discs and horizontal bars are cut from S7 flat stock and sent out for heat treat, or heat-treated at home with a forge/torch and oil quench substitute (less ideal, more variance).
