Silicone Rubber

Soft, wide-temperature-range elastomer used for vibration-isolating mounts, grommets, and wire management rather than armor — deliberately compliant to protect fragile electronics from shock.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightelastomervibration-isolationcomplianceelectronics-protection

Strengths

  • Extremely wide service temperature range
  • Very compliant — excellent vibration/shock isolation for electronics
  • Chemically stable, doesn't degrade quickly

Weaknesses

  • Low tensile/tear strength — not structural
  • Can be expensive per kg for what little material is typically used
  • Poor abrasion resistance versus polyurethane

Notes

Silicone's role in antweights is protecting sensitive components (receivers, flight controllers, battery leads) from the sharp mechanical shock of a weapon impact transmitted through the chassis — a small silicone grommet or standoff pad under a receiver is deliberate, engineered compliance, not an oversight.
