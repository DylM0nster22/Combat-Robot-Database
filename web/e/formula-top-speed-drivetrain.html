Top speed from motor RPM, gear ratio, and wheel diameter

Converts a drive motor's raw (ungeared) no-load RPM through the gearbox ratio and wheel size into the robot's theoretical top ground speed.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightdrivetraintop-speedgear-ratio

Expression

v_top = (rpm_motor / gear_ratio / 60) * pi * D_wheel

Variables

v_toptheoretical top speed, m/s
rpm_motordrive motor's own (ungeared) no-load RPM at the battery's nominal voltage
gear_ratiogearbox reduction, dimensionless (output turns once per this many motor turns)
D_wheelwheel diameter, m

Worked example

Drive motor no-load rpm_motor = 11,000 rpm at 7.4V (2S), gear_ratio = 20:1, wheel diameter D_wheel = 0.032 m (32 mm). Output shaft speed = 11000/20 = 550 rpm. v_top = (550/60) pi 0.032 = 9.167 * 0.1005 = 0.921 m/s (~3.02 ft/s, ~2.06 mph) -- a typical, controllable antweight top speed.
