Aluminum 6061-T6

General-purpose structural aluminum alloy (magnesium/silicon), weaker than 7075 but weldable, cheaper, and easier to source — the default aluminum for antweight frames and brackets.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightaluminumchassisstructure

Strengths

  • Cheap and everywhere
  • Weldable
  • Easy to machine on hobby-grade equipment
  • Anodizes well

Weaknesses

  • ~45% the yield strength of 7075
  • Too soft/thin-dents easily as outer spinner armor

Notes

6061 is the practical default aluminum for internal antweight structure — plate frames, motor plates, standoffs — anywhere 7075's extra strength isn't worth its cost and reduced weldability.
