Tattu 300mAh 2S 75C LiHV

LiHV version of Tattu's popular 300mAh micro pack, charged to 4.35V/cell (7.6V nominal, 8.7V full) instead of the standard 4.2V/cell, giving roughly 4-8% more usable energy in the same size and weight.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightlipolihv2ssmall-format

Strengths

  • More energy and higher rate in the same physical size as standard LiPo
  • Higher continuous voltage under load helps weapon RPM hold up

Weaknesses

  • Needs an LiHV-capable (4.35V/cell) charger profile or it will be undercharged
  • Slightly shorter cycle life than standard LiPo at the same charge/discharge rate
  • Must not be charged on a standard 4.20V/cell LiPo-only charger setting
