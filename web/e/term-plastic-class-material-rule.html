Plastic-Class Material Restriction

The defining rule of plastic antweight/plastic beetleweight: the entire structure must be built from an approved list of 3D-printable/injection-moldable plastics, excluding electronics, motors, fasteners, and bearings. This keeps cost and damage low, which is why plastic antweight is recommended as a first build.

Notes

Approved under SPARC: PLA, PLA+, ABS, PETG, ASA, PET. Banned: TPU, nylon, and any fiber-reinforced/composite filament (carbon-fiber or fiberglass loaded).
