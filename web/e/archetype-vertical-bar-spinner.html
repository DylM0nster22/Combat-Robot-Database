Vertical Bar Spinner

A long bar spinning on a horizontal axis. Nearly all the stored energy sits in the two tips, giving very high energy for the mass.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightspinnerverticalbar

Strengths

  • Mass concentrated at the tips maximises moment of inertia per gram
  • A long bar reaches past an opponent's wedge better than a small disc
  • Simple to make: bar stock, two teeth, a hub

Weaknesses

  • Long bar means a long spin-up and large gyroscopic effects
  • A bent bar after a hard hit becomes an unbalanced bar, which destroys bearings
  • Harder to armour the weapon arc than a compact disc

Notes

Bar versus disc at the same mass is a trade of energy against spin-up and robustness. A bar of length L about its centre has I = mL^2/12, so doubling length quadruples inertia at constant mass. That is free energy on paper, and a longer spin-up plus a bigger gyro problem in practice.
