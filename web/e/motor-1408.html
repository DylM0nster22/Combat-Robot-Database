1408 brushless motor (weapon)

14mm x 8mm stator motor, one of the most popular 'toothpick' drone sizes; a very common choice for 1 lb antweight vertical spinners, small drums, and light bars where a 1806 is too heavy.

Componentplastic-antweightantweightweapon-motorsize-ladderoutrunnertoothpick

Strengths

  • Tall stator (8mm) gives real torque for its size, good spin-up
  • 12x12mm M2 mount pattern common and easy to adapt
  • Cheap, sold in bulk by every FPV motor vendor as 2.5in toothpick motors

Weaknesses

  • Bearings are still drone-spec, not shock-rated for hard weapon impacts
  • Bell can loosen/separate under repeated impact loading if not threadlocked
