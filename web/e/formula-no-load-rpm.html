No-load RPM from Kv and voltage

The speed a motor spins at with no mechanical load and no current-draw losses -- an idealized upper bound. Real no-load speed under actual bearing/brush/air-drag friction is slightly lower than this.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightelectricalkvno-load-speed

Expression

rpm_no_load = Kv * V

Variables

rpm_no_loadno-load rotational speed, rpm
Kvmotor velocity constant, rpm/V
Vapplied voltage, V

Worked example

Kv = 2750 rpm/V at V = 11.1 V (3S): rpm_no_load = 2750 11.1 = 30,525 rpm (omega_nl = 30525 2*pi/60 = 3196 rad/s). Actual free-spin speed will be a few percent lower once bearing drag and air resistance are subtracted.
