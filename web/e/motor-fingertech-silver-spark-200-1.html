FingerTech Silver Spark 16mm Gearmotor, 200:1

One of the highest stock-ratio Silver Sparks, used for slow, high-torque applications like weapon lifters, self-righters, or extremely traction-limited pushing bots rather than main drive.

Strengths

Weaknesses
