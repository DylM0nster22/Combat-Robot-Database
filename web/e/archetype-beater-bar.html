Beater Bar

A simple bar spinning on a horizontal axis at the front — a drum's geometry without a drum's fabrication cost.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightspinnerverticalbeginnerbudget

Strengths

  • Cheapest real weapon to build — bar stock, a hub and a motor
  • Good bite from the low horizontal-axis mounting
  • Forgiving: a beater bar that takes a hit usually still works

Weaknesses

  • Much lower energy than a proper drum or disc of the same mass
  • Little reach; you must close the distance to use it
  • Easy to bend

Notes

The beater bar is the standard first weapon for a new antweight or plastic ant builder. It teaches spinner driving, bite and spin-up management without the cost or the balancing precision of a disc, and it is genuinely competitive at club level.
