Floor interaction (undercutters)

The defining trait of undercutter weapons: the spinning weapon sits low, near or partly below the drive wheels, so it can get underneath an opponent's wedge or skirt where an overhead weapon cannot reach. This close floor proximity means undercutters take more incidental damage from arena floor imperfections and screws, generate sparks/debris that can be a spectator and rules concern, and generally need a slightly more durable, lower running weapon speed than an equivalent overhead design to survive routine floor contact.
