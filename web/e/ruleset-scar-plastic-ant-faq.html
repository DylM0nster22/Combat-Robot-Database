SCAR Plastic Ant FAQ

A supplementary FAQ document SCAR publishes alongside its main ruleset that names specific approved filament brands and explicitly bans several materials (TPU, nylon, composites, epoxy coatings) that are ambiguous under the base ruleset's plain-language material list.

Notes

IMPORTANT ORG-VARIANCE FINDING #1: SCAR is one of the stricter interpreters of the plastic-only rule on MATERIALS — it explicitly bans TPU and nylon by name, materials that are commonly praised in general antweight/beetleweight circles as tough flexible armor. Builders moving a design between events must check the local FAQ, not just the base material list, since 'PETG/PLA/ABS' alone does not settle whether TPU trim or nylon gears are legal.

IMPORTANT ORG-VARIANCE FINDING #2 (verified against both primary sources directly): SCAR's FAQ explicitly ALLOWS 'annealing or smoothing with heat or acid,' while SPARC's current v1.6 Robot Construction Specifications (§16.3.4) explicitly BANS annealing, acetone soaking, and boiling as illegal 'durability-enhancing' post-processing. This is a direct, confirmed contradiction between two real, currently-published plastic-ant rulesets on the exact same question — a concrete case where a part legal at one event is illegal at another with no visual difference at inspection time, entirely dependent on which ruleset the event runs.
