Western Allied Robotics (WAR) Plastic Antweight — Original 2015 Rules

The founding ruleset of the plastic antweight class, introduced by Western Allied Robotics in 2015, restricting the chassis and weapon to PET/PETG/ABS/PLA/PLA+ while exempting motors, electronics, axles, fasteners and adhesives.

Notes

This WAR wording is essentially word-for-word what became SPARC section 4.4, making WAR the direct ancestor of nearly every plastic-ant ruleset now in use.
