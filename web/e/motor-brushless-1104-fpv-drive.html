1104-size Brushless Motor (FPV-derived) for Custom Drive Pods

An 11mm-diameter, 4mm-tall outrunner brushless motor, originally developed for micro FPV drones, repurposed by combat robot builders into custom or off-the-shelf (e.g. Repeat Robotics) 16mm drive gearboxes for antweight main drive.

Some figures on this page could not be verified against a primary source. Treat them as indicative and check the manufacturer before relying on them.

Strengths

  • Very high power-to-weight ratio compared to a brushed motor of similar size
  • No brushes to wear out
  • Wide range of kv (winding) options available from FPV motor market

Weaknesses

  • Sensorless variants can cog or stutter at very low speed/duty cycle, hurting fine driving control
  • Requires a reversing brushless ESC rather than a simple brushed driver
  • Needs to be built into or bought as part of a gearbox — not a drop-in drive-and-wheel solution by itself

Notes

kv commonly seen in this application is in the 3000-6000kv range depending on the specific winding chosen by the seller/builder; Repeat Robotics' early Mk3/Mk4 drive pod used a custom-wound ~3500kv 1104 motor.
