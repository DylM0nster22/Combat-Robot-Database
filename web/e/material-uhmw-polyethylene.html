UHMW polyethylene (plastic-antweight weapon body)

Ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene sheet or rod, a tough, low-friction, impact-resistant plastic that is a legal weapon and structural material for plastic-antweight classes where metal weapon bodies are restricted; used for discs, drum bodies, and wedge scoops.

Notes

UHMW is legal and popular specifically because plastic-antweight rulesets generally restrict the bulk weapon/structure material to non-metal while still allowing small hardened metal inserts, fasteners, motors, and bearings -- check the exact event ruleset for current material limits.
