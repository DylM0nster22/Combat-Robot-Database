Voltage Sag from Internal Resistance

Estimates how far pack voltage will drop under a given current draw using the pack's internal resistance, the direct cause-and-effect behind weapon-spin-up brownouts in small combat robots.

Formulavoltage-saginternal-resistancebrownout

Expression

V_sag = I_draw * R_internal;  V_under_load = V_nominal - V_sag

Variables

V_sagvoltage drop under load, volts
I_drawinstantaneous current draw, amps
R_internalpack internal resistance, ohms (a fresh small antweight LiPo pack is roughly 0.015-0.04 ohm per cell; multiply per cell for series count, and note resistance rises with age/damage/cold)
V_under_loadactual voltage seen by the ESC/receiver while the load is applied
V_nominalpack's resting/nominal voltage before the load hit

Worked example

A used 3S pack with internal resistance of 0.03 ohm/cell (0.09 ohm total for the 3S series stack) hit with a combined weapon-spinup-plus-drive spike of 25A: V_sag = 25 * 0.09 = 2.25V. Starting from 11.1V nominal, the pack momentarily sags to about 8.85V — potentially below the brownout threshold of a receiver or BEC that expects a stable 3S-range input, which is exactly the mechanism behind an RX reset at the moment the weapon motor spins up while the drive motors are also under load.
