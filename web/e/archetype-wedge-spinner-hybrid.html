Wedge + Vertical Spinner Hybrid

A vertical spinner with wedge-shaped forks flanking the weapon, so the robot wins the ground game and still carries damage.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightspinnercontrolmetahybrid

Strengths

  • Removes the vertical spinner's single biggest weakness — losing the wedge war
  • The forks feed opponents into the weapon rather than letting them ride over
  • Dominant in the modern antweight meta

Weaknesses

  • Packaging is tight: forks, weapon and drive all compete for the front of the robot
  • The forks must not foul the weapon arc
  • Heavier than a pure spinner, so weapon energy is slightly reduced

Notes

This is the defining configuration of modern competitive antweight. A bare vertical spinner loses to a wedge; a bare wedge loses to anything that can out-control it. Bolting low hinged forks either side of a vertical weapon means the robot wins the initial engagement and then applies damage. Almost every top 1 lb design converges on some version of this.
