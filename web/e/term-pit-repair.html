Five-Minute Pit Repair

The plastic-ant repair philosophy of designing bolt-on, single-piece printed sub-assemblies so that a cracked wedge, wheel, or weapon can be swapped for a spare printed at home in under five minutes between matches, rather than requiring on-site fabrication or welding as a metal repair might.

Termplastic-antweightantweightrepairabilitydesign-patternpit-strategystrategy

Notes

The realistic window at a busy event is ten to twenty minutes. That is enough to swap a wheel, replace a weapon, re-solder a wire, change a battery or straighten a wedge. It is not enough to re-print a chassis or rebuild a gearbox. Design for it: modular bolt-on armour, spare weapons already balanced, and pre-made wiring harnesses.
