Plastic Antweight

A 1 lb (454 g) variant of antweight in which the entire robot (except electronics, motors, and fasteners) must be built from approved 3D-printable/thermoplastic materials. Started by Western Allied Robotics to make antweight building cheaper and more accessible, later folded into SPARC as an official weight-class variant. It is the fastest-growing beginner class because a full robot can be printed on a hobby FDM printer for well under $50.

Notes

Allowed materials under SPARC: PLA, PLA+, ABS, PETG, ASA, and PET. Banned: TPU, nylon, and any fiber-filled/composite plastic (carbon-fiber or fiberglass-loaded filament), and any metal structural part beyond fasteners, motors, bearings, and electronics. This keeps damage rates and repair costs very low, which is exactly why it is recommended as the first build for new competitors.
