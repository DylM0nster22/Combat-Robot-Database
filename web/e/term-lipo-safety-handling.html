LiPo Battery Safety Handling

A set of near-universal pit-safety practices for lithium-polymer batteries used in combat robots: charge only in a fireproof LiPo bag or vented metal/ammo box, never leave a charging battery unattended, inspect for swelling/puncture damage before every use, and store/transport in fire-resistant bags. Several major events (e.g. RoboGames 2017) have had LiPo fires in the pits, which is why fireproof bags are now commonly mandatory rather than optional.

Notes

Arenas/venues are commonly required to be ventilated for roughly one full air exchange every 60 seconds, and pit areas should have a clear evacuation path in case of a battery fire.
