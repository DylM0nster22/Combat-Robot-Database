Rotational kinetic energy stored in a spinning weapon

The total kinetic energy stored in the weapon at a given spin speed -- the number combat robotics people mean when they say a weapon 'has X joules'.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightrotationalkinetic-energycore-formula

Expression

KE = 0.5 * I * omega^2

Variables

KEstored rotational kinetic energy, J
Itotal weapon moment of inertia about its spin axis, kg*m^2
omegaweapon angular velocity, rad/s

Worked example

Using the compound weapon I_total = 3.25e-5 kgm^2 spinning at omega = 2094.4 rad/s (20,000 rpm): KE = 0.5 3.25e-5 2094.4^2 = 0.5 3.25e-5 4,386,506 = 0.5 142.56 = 71.3 J stored.
