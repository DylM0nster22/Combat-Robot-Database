Sheet/panel weight from area, thickness, and material density

Basic mass-from-volume-and-density calculation, used constantly when picking armor or frame stock thickness against the weight budget.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightweight-and-cgmaterialssheet-stock

Expression

weight = area * thickness * density

Variables

weightmass of the sheet/panel, g (if density is g/cm^3 and area/thickness are in cm)
areapanel area, cm^2
thicknesspanel thickness, cm
densitymaterial density, g/cm^3

Worked example

HDPE (plastic-antweight-legal) panel: area = 100 cm^2 (10cm x 10cm), thickness = 0.6 cm (6mm), density = 0.95 g/cm^3. weight = 100 0.6 0.95 = 57.0 g. For comparison, the same 100 cm^2 panel in 6061 aluminum (density 2.70 g/cm^3) at a thinner 0.3 cm (3mm): weight = 100 0.3 2.70 = 81.0 g -- illustrating why plastic-class frames need much greater thickness to compete structurally, but still often land lighter or comparable per panel once thickness is matched to stiffness needs.
