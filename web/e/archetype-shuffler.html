Shuffler

A robot using cams or eccentric linkages to shuffle along, qualifying for a partial non-wheeled weight bonus at many events.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightwalkerbonus

Strengths

  • Earns a weight bonus at most events without a full walking linkage
  • Simpler than a true walker

Weaknesses

  • Smaller bonus than a true walker
  • Slow and imprecise to drive
  • Some rulesets classify shufflers as wheeled and give no bonus at all

Notes

Shufflers sit between wheels and true walkers. Because classification varies by organisation, the bonus is the first thing to confirm — a shuffler judged to be wheeled is simply a slow robot with no compensation.
