Plastic Antweight

A 1 lb (454 g) combat robot weight class in which structure, armor and weapons must be built from unmodified 3D-printable plastics (PLA/PLA+/PETG/ABS/ASA/PET), with metal permitted only for fasteners, motors, bearings, shafts, electronics and adhesives that do not themselves act as armor or weapon impactors.

Notes

Originated with Western Allied Robotics (WAR) in 2015 as an intentionally weaker-material 1lb class so that new builders could compete without CNC machining or metal fabrication. SPARC later formalized 'Plastic Classes' (section 4.4 of the SPARC Robot Construction Specifications) as a template most North American regional events now copy verbatim or with minor local amendments. It is functionally the same 454 g weight limit as a normal antweight; the only difference is the construction-material restriction. Some clubs also run a lighter plastic fairyweight/150g class, especially in the UK, which is a distinct weight bracket, not the same as the 1lb US plastic ant.
