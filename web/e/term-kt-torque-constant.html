Kt (torque constant)

A motor's Kt rating describes how much torque it produces per amp of current. The physically exact relation to Kv is Kt[N*m/A] = 9.5493/Kv[rpm/V]; a widely-used hobby shorthand, Kt = 8.27/Kv, bakes in an assumed ~87% efficiency and gives a lower number than the exact form. See formula-kt-from-kv.
