N20 Micro Gearmotor (6-12V)

A tiny brushed DC gearmotor in a roughly 12x10x10mm metal gearbox, the most common drive motor in plastic antweights due to its low cost, light weight, and wide range of stock gear ratios.

Componentplastic-antweightdrivebudgetbeginner

Strengths

  • Very cheap and widely available
  • Metal gearbox is more durable than plastic-gear toy motors
  • Many stock gear ratios (from ~30 RPM crawlers to 1000+ RPM speedsters)

Weaknesses

  • Low torque compared to bigger motors, limits ramming power
  • Output shaft can shear under sudden weapon-strike shock loads if used for anything but drive
