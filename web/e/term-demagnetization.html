Demagnetization (weapon motor failure)

Permanent partial loss of magnetic strength in a motor's rotor magnets, caused by driving current far beyond the motor's rated stall current (common during a hard weapon-vs-weapon or weapon-vs-wall impact stall) or by sustained overheating past the magnet's Curie-adjacent temperature limit. A demagnetized motor permanently loses torque and top KV-equivalent speed even after the fault current stops.

Notes

Small FPV-derived weapon motors are especially vulnerable because their magnets are thin and cheap; a single violent stall against a wedge or wall, or a jammed weapon, can push instantaneous current to several times rated stall current for a few milliseconds -- enough to demagnetize without ever tripping a slower-reacting ESC current limit.
