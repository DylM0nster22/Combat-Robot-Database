Rake angle

The angle of a tooth's leading face relative to a purely radial line from the center of rotation. Zero rake (radial) gives the most direct, highest peak-force impact; a swept-back (negative) rake trades some peak force and bite depth for a longer, gentler engagement and less shock loading on the tooth root and bearings; a forward (positive/aggressive) rake can dig in harder but is far more prone to chipping or snapping on a hard hit.
