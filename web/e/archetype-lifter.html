Lifter

A powered arm that pivots upward to lift an opponent's front off the floor, removing their traction and control.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcontrollifter

Strengths

  • Controls opponents without needing to damage them
  • Slow, controllable lift is better for carrying an opponent than a flipper's single throw
  • Can hold an opponent up for the whole match, which scores heavily on control

Weaknesses

  • The arm is a lever that transmits impact straight into its pivot
  • Needs a wedge-quality edge to get under in the first place
  • Slow — a spinner will hit you during the lift

Notes

A lifter is a wedge with a follow-through. The hard part is not the lifting, it is the getting under: a lifter whose leading edge is not as good as a dedicated wedge's never gets to use its arm.
