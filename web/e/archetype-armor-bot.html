Defensive / Armour-Only Bot

A robot built purely to survive, betting that the opponent breaks itself or runs out of time.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcontroldefensive

Strengths

  • Spinners frequently destroy themselves on heavy armour
  • Simple, cheap and very hard to knock out
  • An excellent way to learn what actually survives

Weaknesses

  • Wins almost nothing on aggression or damage, so decisions go against you
  • Boring to drive and to watch

Notes

A pure armour bot exploits the fact that a spinner's energy has to go somewhere. Against a hard, compliant, well-braced target, much of it goes back into the spinner. It rarely wins tournaments but it is an instructive build and a genuine counter to fragile high-energy opponents.

Strong against

  • Horizontal Bar Spinner
  • Full-Body Shell Spinner
  • Vertical Disc Spinner

Weak against

  • Control Bot
  • Grabber / Clamper
  • Wedge
