Battery energy capacity in watt-hours

Converts a battery's amp-hour capacity and nominal voltage into total stored energy in watt-hours, useful for comparing packs across different cell counts (voltages) on an equal-energy basis.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightelectricalbatteryenergy-capacity

Expression

Wh = V_nominal * Capacity_Ah

Variables

Whstored energy, watt-hours
V_nominalbattery nominal voltage, V (2S LiPo = 7.4V, 3S = 11.1V)
Capacity_Ahbattery capacity, Ah

Worked example

300 mAh (0.3 Ah) 2S (7.4V) LiPo: Wh = 7.4 * 0.3 = 2.22 Wh.
