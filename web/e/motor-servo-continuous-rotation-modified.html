Modified Continuous-Rotation 9g Micro Servo (Drive)

A standard 9g hobby micro servo (e.g. SG90-class) with its position-limiting potentiometer removed or bypassed (replaced with a fixed voltage divider) so it spins continuously instead of to a commanded angle, letting the transmitter's stick position set speed and direction directly with zero external electronics.

Strengths

  • No separate ESC/driver needed — plugs straight into a receiver channel
  • Extremely cheap and small, ideal for gram-constrained plastic antweights
  • Simple to source and modify with widely available tutorials

Weaknesses

  • Low top speed and low torque compared to a dedicated gearmotor + ESC of similar weight
  • Modification quality varies (pot removal/centering must be done carefully or the "stopped" position drifts)
  • Internal gearbox (plastic in cheap servos) is not built for combat shock loads

Specifications

weight g9
voltage nominal v5

Notes

The standard mod: remove the servo's positional potentiometer and replace it with two fixed resistors (dividing voltage to sit at the servo's centered/neutral setpoint), and often trim/cut a mechanical stop tab inside the gearbox so the output gear can rotate freely. A 1ms pulse spins one direction, 2ms the other, ~1.5ms stops it, mirroring standard RC PWM servo signal ranges.
