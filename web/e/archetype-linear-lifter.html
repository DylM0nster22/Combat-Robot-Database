Linear Actuator Lifter

A lifter driven by a leadscrew or linear actuator rather than a rotating arm, giving high force at low speed.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcontrollifteradvanced

Strengths

  • Very high force for the power consumed
  • Holds position without drawing current, so you can pin indefinitely
  • Compact compared to a long arm

Weaknesses

  • Slow — seconds per stroke
  • Leadscrews jam when bent or contaminated
  • Complex to build at 1 lb

Notes

Linear actuators give a 1 lb robot genuine crushing or lifting force because a screw multiplies torque enormously. The price is speed: an actuator that can lift a robot takes a second or more to do it, which is a long time in a match.
