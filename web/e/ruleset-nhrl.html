NHRL Open Rules (National Havoc Robot League)

NHRL runs the largest recurring US combat robotics series (Franklin, MA) across exactly three weight classes: 3lb (beetleweight), 12lb (hobbyweight), and 30lb (featherweight). It uses a qualifying-then-bracket tournament format, mandatory weapon locks and radio failsafe testing, and pays cash prizes, which distinguishes it from most hobbyist-only events.
