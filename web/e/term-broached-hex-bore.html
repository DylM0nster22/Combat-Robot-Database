Broached hex bore

A hub or pulley bored with an internal hexagonal hole that slides onto a matching hexagonal section of shaft, giving a positive, low-part-count torque connection without a separate key. Very common on antweight weapon pulleys and sprockets because a short length of hex stock (or a shaft with a hex section machined/ground onto it) is cheap and simple, and the hex engagement handles shock loads much better than a round-bore set-screw hub, though it still needs some axial retention (a screw, retaining ring, or shoulder) to keep the hub from sliding off the shaft.
