Moment of inertia — thin-walled cylinder / tube about its long axis

MOI of a thin cylindrical shell (a tube or drum's outer wall) spinning about its own central longitudinal axis. Mathematically identical in form to the thin-ring case (all wall mass sits at radius r regardless of the tube's length), and is the right model for a hollow drum-spinner shell or a shaft/hub.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightrotationalmoiweapon-inertia

Expression

I = m * r^2

Variables

Imoment of inertia, kg*m^2
mmass of the cylindrical shell/wall, kg
rradius of the cylindrical wall, m

Worked example

A drum-spinner's hardened-steel outer shell (thin wall, ignoring the end plates) has mass m = 0.040 kg (40 g) and radius r = 0.020 m (20 mm). I = 0.040 0.020^2 = 0.040 0.0004 = 0.000016 kgm^2 (1.6e-5 kgm^2).
