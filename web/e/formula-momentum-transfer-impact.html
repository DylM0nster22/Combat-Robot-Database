Momentum transfer to the opponent on impact

Models a weapon strike as a partially-inelastic collision between the weapon's effective mass (at the tip) and the opponent's total mass, to estimate how fast the opponent gets thrown/launched. Coefficient of restitution e is empirical: roughly 0.2-0.3 for a mostly-absorbed/deforming hit, 0.4-0.5 for a stiffer, more elastic bounce-off hit.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightrotationalimpactmomentum

Expression

delta_v_opponent = (1 + e) * m_eff * v_tip / (m_eff + m_opponent)

Variables

delta_v_opponentvelocity imparted to the opponent by the hit, m/s
ecoefficient of restitution, dimensionless (0 = perfectly inelastic, 1 = perfectly elastic)
m_effweapon's effective mass at the tip, kg (see formula-effective-mass-at-tooth)
v_tipweapon tip speed just before impact, m/s
m_opponentmass of the struck opponent robot, kg

Worked example

m_eff = 0.03174 kg, v_tip = 67.0 m/s, hitting a full 1 lb opponent m_opponent = 0.454 kg, e = 0.4: delta_v = (1.4) 0.03174 67.0 / (0.03174 + 0.454) = (1.4 * 2.127) / 0.4857 = 2.978 / 0.4857 = 6.13 m/s -- the opponent can be launched/kicked at roughly 6 m/s from a solid hit if it is free to move.

Notes

Coefficient of restitution for robot-on-robot hits is not rigorously measured in the hobby; treat e as a tunable estimate, not a precise material constant.
