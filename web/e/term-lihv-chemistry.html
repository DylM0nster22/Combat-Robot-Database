LiHV (High-Voltage LiPo)

A LiPo cell chemistry variant built to be charged safely to 4.35V per cell instead of the standard 4.20V, giving roughly 5-10% more stored energy in the same physical cell at the cost of slightly reduced cycle life and the requirement for an LiHV-aware charger.
