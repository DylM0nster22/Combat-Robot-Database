M3 flat head cap screw

M3 machine screw used throughout antweight construction.

Componentantweightplastic-antweight

Strengths

  • Standard and cheap
  • Available in grade 12.9 alloy steel

Weaknesses

  • Backs out under vibration without threadlocker
  • Stainless versions are noticeably weaker in shear

Specifications

threadM3

Notes

Sits flush, which matters on a wedge face or any surface an opponent slides across. Use grade 12.9 alloy steel, not stainless, anywhere the fastener carries load. Stainless A2/A4 is roughly half the tensile strength and shears where 12.9 holds. Blue threadlocker on everything.
