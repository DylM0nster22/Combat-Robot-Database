Annealing (3D Print Post-Processing)

A post-print heat-treatment process (baking a printed part at a temperature below its melt point, sometimes packed in sand or salt to limit warping) that can meaningfully increase a part's layer strength and heat resistance; explicitly permitted post-processing under SCAR's plastic ant FAQ.
