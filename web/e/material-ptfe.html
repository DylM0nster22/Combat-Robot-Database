PTFE (Teflon)

Extremely low-friction, chemically inert fluoropolymer with poor mechanical strength — used narrowly for low-friction slip surfaces/bushings, essentially never for armor or structure.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightlow-frictionbushingsniche

Strengths

  • Lowest friction coefficient of common plastics
  • Extremely chemically inert and heat resistant
  • Self-lubricating

Weaknesses

  • Very low tensile/yield strength and stiffness — useless structurally or as armor
  • Cold-flows (creeps) under load
  • Expensive per kg for what little structural benefit it gives
  • Essentially unbondable

Notes

PTFE's role in antweights is limited to friction-reduction (occasional slip washers or wire sleeving) rather than any structural or armor role — its mechanical weakness rules it out for anything load-bearing.
