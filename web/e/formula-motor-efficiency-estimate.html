Motor efficiency estimate at an operating point

Ratio of useful mechanical power out to electrical power in, at a specific torque/speed operating point -- NOT a constant, it varies across the motor's speed range and is generally best near (but not exactly at) the peak-power point.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightelectricalefficiencymotor-sizing

Expression

eta = P_out / P_in = (T * omega) / (V * I)

Variables

etaefficiency at this operating point, dimensionless (0-1)
Ttorque delivered at this operating point, N*m
omegaangular velocity at this operating point, rad/s
Vapplied voltage, V
Icurrent drawn at this operating point, A

Worked example

At the 20,000 rpm (omega = 2094.4 rad/s) operating point: T = 0.03595 Nm (from formula-power-curve-peak-power), V = 11.1 V. Current at that torque: I = T/Kt = 0.03595/0.003472 = 10.36 A. P_out = Tomega = 0.035952094.4 = 75.3 W. P_in = VI = 11.1*10.36 = 115.0 W. eta = 75.3/115.0 = 0.655 -> 65.5% efficient at this operating point.
