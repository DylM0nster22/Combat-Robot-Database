GNB 1S 450mAh 4.35V HV

Single-cell LiHV pack, too low-voltage to run most antweight drivetrains alone but a real-world example of the small HV cells sometimes used to power an isolated receiver, telemetry, or a low-voltage weapon controller board in a combat robot's electronics bay.

Notes

1S packs are not used as the main pack in antweights (too low voltage for typical brushed/brushless drive), but the same category of small 1S/2S HV or LiFe cell is what builders reach for when isolating receiver power from a sagging main weapon battery.
