Small-Diameter Steel Weapon Shaft

A short length of steel drill rod or precision shaft used to carry a plastic weapon disc/drum, exempt from the plastic-only rule as an 'axle' under SPARC-derived language as long as it does not itself act as an armor surface or weapon edge.

Componentplastic-antweightshaftweaponmetal-exemption

Strengths

  • Much stiffer and more wear-resistant than a printed shaft would be
  • Explicitly allowed as an axle exemption in every ruleset reviewed

Weaknesses

  • Must be fully shrouded by the printed weapon body so it can't be argued to be acting as a metal weapon tip
