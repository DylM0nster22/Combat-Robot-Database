RoboWars Australia Weight Classes & Rules

The Australian combat robotics scene (RoboWars Australia) follows essentially the same SPARC-style weight ladder used in the US and Canada, not the lighter UK/AWS scale: Antweight is 1 lb (454 g), matching US antweight rather than the UK's 150 g class. Australia's ladder additionally runs a Super Heavyweight class at 340 lb (154 kg), one tier above the common 220 lb heavyweight, plus a separate off-arena 'UltiBot' outdoor-terrain division.
