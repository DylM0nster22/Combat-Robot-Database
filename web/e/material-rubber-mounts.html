Rubber Grommets / Standoff Mounts

Small rubber (neoprene/nitrile) grommets and standoffs used to isolate PCBs (receivers, ESCs, flight controllers) from a rigid chassis — a deliberate-compliance countermeasure against shock-induced component failure.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightelastomercomplianceelectronics-mounting

Strengths

  • Cheap, off-the-shelf, easy to install
  • Effective, simple shock isolation for small PCBs
  • Wide range of durometers/sizes commercially available

Weaknesses

  • Adds a small amount of assembly complexity/weight versus rigid standoffs
  • Can degrade/harden over time with heat or age

Notes

Rigid metal standoffs transmit chassis-borne shock directly into a PCB's solder joints and connector pins; swapping to a rubber-grommeted standoff at the same mounting points is one of the cheapest, lowest-weight reliability upgrades available in an antweight, trading a fraction of a gram of rigidity for a large reduction in electronics failure rate after hard hits.
