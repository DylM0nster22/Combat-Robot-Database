G10 / FR4 Fiberglass Laminate

Woven fiberglass cloth impregnated with epoxy resin and compressed into rigid sheet — very stiff, hard, and abrasion resistant (notoriously tool-dulling to cut), used for rigid armor backers and structural plates.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightcompositefiberglassrigid-platetool-dulling

Strengths

  • Very stiff and hard for its weight
  • Good electrical insulator (FR4 grade is the same material used for PCB substrates)
  • Dimensionally stable, doesn't creep like plastics
  • Reasonably tough — resists cracking better than acrylic or CF

Weaknesses

  • Extremely abrasive to cut/drill — chews through cheap bits fast
  • Glass dust from cutting/sanding is a health hazard, requires PPE/extraction
  • Heavier than an equivalent 3D-printed shell

Notes

G10 is a popular internal frame/backer material behind a softer armor skin (UHMW or TPU) because it adds real stiffness without titanium's cost, at the price of being miserable to machine with hobby tools.
