Heat Treating

Hardening a steel weapon part by heating and quenching, then tempering it back to toughness.

Termantweightplastic-antweightfabrication

Notes

A weapon that is fully hard is also brittle and will chip rather than deform. The usual target for combat weapon steel is a compromise hardness — hard enough to keep an edge, tough enough to survive impact. Home heat-treating small parts is possible with a torch and quench oil but temperature control is poor; for anything important, buying pre-hardened stock or sending the part out gives far more consistent results.
