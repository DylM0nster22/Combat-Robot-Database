Weapon Balancing

Removing or adding mass so a spinning weapon's centre of mass sits on its axis.

Termantweightplastic-antweightfabrication

Notes

An unbalanced antweight weapon at 20,000 rpm generates enough vibration to back fasteners out across the whole robot, destroy bearings within a match, and make the robot undriveable. Static-balance a weapon by resting its shaft on two level parallel edges and letting it settle — the heavy side rolls down. Remove material from the heavy side with a drill rather than adding it to the light side. Re-balance after every significant impact; a bent weapon is an unbalanced weapon.
