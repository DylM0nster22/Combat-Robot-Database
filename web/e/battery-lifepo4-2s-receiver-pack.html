2S LiFePO4 Receiver/Backup Pack

A small 2-cell LiFePO4 ("LiFe") pack at 6.6V nominal (3.3V/cell), the classic RC receiver-pack chemistry: much flatter discharge curve and far more abuse-tolerant than LiPo, occasionally used by antweight builders to power an isolated receiver or weapon-control board so it never browns out even if the main LiPo sags hard under weapon load.

Notes

LiFePO4 has lower energy density than LiPo (roughly 90-110 Wh/kg vs 150-200+ Wh/kg for LiPo) so it is not competitive as a main antweight power source, but its flat voltage curve and much lower fire risk make it a sensible choice specifically for a dedicated, isolated receiver/electronics rail. Capacity/weight given are representative of small RC LiFe RX packs, not a single named product.
