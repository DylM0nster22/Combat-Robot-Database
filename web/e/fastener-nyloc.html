Nyloc nut

A nut with a nylon insert that grips the thread and resists vibration loosening.

Componentantweightplastic-antweight

Strengths

  • Genuinely resists backing out
  • Reusable a few times

Weaknesses

  • Taller than a plain nut
  • Loses grip after repeated use
  • Nylon softens if it gets hot

Specifications

common sizesM2, M3

Notes

Nylocs are the standard antweight answer to vibration. They are not a substitute for threadlocker on a screw going into a tapped hole — they only work where you have a through-bolt and room for the nut.
