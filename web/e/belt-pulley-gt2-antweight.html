GT2 Belt & Pulley Drivetrain Set (antweight scale)

A small GT2 (2mm pitch) timing belt and matching aluminum or 3D-printed pulley set used to link a front and rear wheel on each side of a 4WD antweight, letting two motors drive four wheels instead of needing four separate drive motors.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightbelt-pulley4wddrivetraingt2

Strengths

  • Gives 4-wheel traction and redundancy (surviving a lifted/high-centered wheel) using only 2 drive motors
  • GT2 belts are cheap, widely available (from 3D printer supply chains), and don't require the precision of chain
  • Printed pulleys keep the added weight low

Weaknesses

  • Adds mechanical complexity and a few extra failure points (belt tension, pulley wear, belt skipping teeth under shock)
  • Extra weight vs a simple 2WD direct-drive layout, competing with armor/weapon weight budget
  • Belt can be damaged by direct weapon hits if not shielded

Notes

A common way to get 4WD traction and pushing power on a 1 lb control bot without the weight and complexity of 4 separate gearmotors and ESCs.
