Pololu Micro Metal Gearmotor HP 6V, 30:1

Pololu's High-Power (HP) tune of its popular micro metal gearmotor line at a fast 30:1 ratio: a 10x12mm all-metal gearbox on a 3mm D-shaft output, favoring speed and current draw over the LP/MP tunes of the same physical motor.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightdrive-motorbrushedpololumicro-metal-gearmotor

Strengths

  • Precision-manufactured, far more consistent unit-to-unit than generic N-series motors
  • Well documented performance curves from Pololu
  • Very compact 10x12mm gearbox cross-section

Weaknesses

  • More expensive than generic N20 motors
  • 3mm output shaft is short (9mm) — needs a hub or coupler, not usually long enough for direct antweight wheel mounting like a Silver Spark
