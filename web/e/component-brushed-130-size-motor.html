130-Size Brushed Drive Motor

A classic small brushed can motor (the same family used in cheap toy cars) sometimes paired with a 3D-printed or small stock gearbox for antweight drive; cheaper and more available than purpose-built combat drive motors.

Strengths

  • Extremely cheap
  • High no-load RPM gives room for a wide range of gear ratios

Weaknesses

  • Needs a separate gearbox (adds weight/complexity) since raw RPM is too high for direct drive
  • Lower quality bearings than purpose-made combat motors
