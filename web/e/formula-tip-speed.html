Weapon tip speed from RPM and diameter

Converts weapon RPM and blade/disc diameter into linear tip speed, the number that actually determines impact energy transfer and 'bite'.

Formulaformulatip-speed

Expression

v_mps = (RPM * pi * D_mm) / 60000  |  v_ftps = v_mps * 3.281  |  v_mph = v_mps * 2.237

Variables

v_mpstip speed, meters/second
RPMweapon rotational speed, revolutions per minute
D_mmfull weapon diameter at the tip, millimeters (NOT radius -- the /60000 already folds in the /2 from radius and the mm-to-m conversion)
v_ftpstip speed, feet/second
v_mphtip speed, miles/hour

Worked example

A 4 in (101.6 mm) vertical disc spinning at 21,000 RPM: v = (21000 pi 101.6) / 60000 = (21000 3.1416 101.6) / 60000 = 6,703,000 / 60000 approx 111.7 m/s = 366 ft/s = 250 mph. This matches the commonly cited antweight vertical-spinner target of roughly 200-250 mph tip speed.
