Tip speed of a spinning weapon

The linear (straight-line) speed of the outermost point of the weapon -- the speed at which the tooth or blade edge is actually moving through the air, which is what does the cutting/hitting.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightrotationaltip-speedcore-formulaformula

Expression

v_tip = omega * r_tip

Variables

v_tiptip speed, m/s
omegaweapon angular velocity, rad/s
r_tipradius from the spin axis to the outermost striking point (including any tooth extension), m

Worked example

Same compound weapon, tooth tip extends out to r_tip = 0.032 m (32 mm) at omega = 2094.4 rad/s: v_tip = 2094.4 0.032 = 67.0 m/s. Converting: 67.0 m/s 3.281 ft/m = 219.8 ft/s (~149.9 mph) -- a hard-hitting but achievable tip speed for a 1 lb vertical spinner.
