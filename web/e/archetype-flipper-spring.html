Spring Flipper

An arm that releases stored spring energy in a single stroke to throw an opponent into the air.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcontrolflipperadvanced

Strengths

  • A successful flip can end a match instantly against a non-invertible robot
  • Beats vertical spinners by attacking them from underneath, where they cannot respond
  • No compressed gas system needed, so it is legal everywhere

Weaknesses

  • One shot, then a slow re-cock
  • Useless against an invertible opponent
  • The cocking mechanism is complex and a common failure point

Notes

Spring flippers are the practical flipper for 1 lb, because pneumatics do not scale down well. The design problem is the latch: it must hold the full spring force, release cleanly on command, and survive being hit. Most antweight flipper failures are latch failures.
