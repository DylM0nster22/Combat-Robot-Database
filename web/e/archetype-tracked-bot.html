Tracked Robot

Tracks instead of wheels, for maximum ground contact and a very low, stable platform.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcontroldrivetrain

Strengths

  • Large contact patch gives excellent pushing traction
  • Naturally low and stable
  • Tracks can absorb hits that would break a wheel

Weaknesses

  • Thrown tracks are a very common and match-ending failure
  • Heavy for the traction gained
  • Steering scrubs and wastes power

Notes

Tracks look ideal for a pushing robot and usually disappoint at 1 lb. A thrown track is an instant loss, and at antweight scale it is hard to build a tensioner robust enough to prevent it. Most builders who try tracks return to wheels.

  • Control Bot

Weak against
