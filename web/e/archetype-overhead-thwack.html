Overhead Thwack

A thwackbot whose tail swings in a vertical plane, striking downward rather than sweeping horizontally.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightimpactbudget

Strengths

  • Strikes the top armour without needing a weapon motor
  • Self-rights naturally
  • Simple and hard to break

Weaknesses

  • Requires sharp drivetrain reversals to build momentum
  • Inconsistent — the arm does not always come over cleanly
  • Low energy

Notes

An overhead thwack uses drivetrain reversal to throw a pivoting arm over the top of the robot. It is a clever way to get an overhead strike for almost no weight, and it is unreliable enough that it is best treated as a secondary threat on a control bot.
