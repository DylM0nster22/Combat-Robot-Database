Moment of inertia — slender rod about its own center (horizontal bar spinner)

MOI of a uniform straight bar rotating about an axis through its own midpoint, perpendicular to its length. This is the standard model for a horizontal (full-width) bar spinner weapon.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightrotationalmoiweapon-inertia

Expression

I = (1/12) * m * L^2

Variables

Imoment of inertia, kg*m^2
mtotal mass of the bar, kg
Ltotal length of the bar (tip to tip), m

Worked example

A horizontal bar-spinner blade has mass m = 0.090 kg (90 g) and total length L = 0.090 m (90 mm, so each tip is 45 mm from center). I = (1/12) 0.090 0.090^2 = (1/12) 0.090 0.0081 = (1/12) 0.000729 = 0.00006075 kgm^2 (6.075e-5 kg*m^2).
