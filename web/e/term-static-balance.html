Static balance

Balancing a rotating part so its center of mass lies exactly on the rotation axis, checked by letting the part rest freely on a level, low-friction axis (a balancing mandrel) and seeing whether it stays put in any orientation or always rotates to put a heavy spot at the bottom. Sufficient for thin, disc-like weapons where essentially all the mass sits in one plane perpendicular to the shaft; the standard, low-cost balancing method for most antweight vertical-disc and shell-spinner builds.
