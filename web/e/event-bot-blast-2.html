Bot Blast

An annual US insectweight-focused combat robotics event running a modified SPARC ruleset. Its main deviations from stock SPARC are a modified double-elimination format, a ban on autonomous robots, an immediate KO (at official discretion) for any robot with an exposed/damaged LiPo battery, and simplified judging criteria versus full SPARC judging.

Notes

The exposed-LiPo-equals-immediate-KO rule is a notable Bot-Blast-specific safety escalation beyond baseline SPARC, directly reflecting how seriously LiPo fire risk is treated in the hobby.
