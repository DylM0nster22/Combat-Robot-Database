Predicting the maximum safe turn rate before gyro-dance tipping

Rearranges the precession-torque and tipping-moment formulas to solve directly for the fastest a driver can yaw the chassis (at a given weapon speed) before the precession reaction exceeds the tipping threshold and the robot starts to rock.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightgyroscopicprecessiondriving-technique

Expression

omega_turn_max = M_tip / (I * omega_weapon)

Variables

omega_turn_maxmaximum safe chassis turning (yaw) rate before tipping, rad/s
M_tiptipping-moment threshold of the chassis, N*m (formula-tipping-moment-threshold)
Iweapon moment of inertia, kg*m^2
omega_weaponweapon angular velocity, rad/s

Worked example

M_tip = 0.2004 Nm, I = 3.25e-5 kgm^2, omega_weapon = 2094.4 rad/s (20,000 rpm weapon speed): omega_turn_max = 0.2004 / (3.25e-5 * 2094.4) = 0.2004 / 0.06807 = 2.944 rad/s (~28.1 deg per 60ms tick, or about 169 deg/s, ~0.47 rev/s). A driver should keep in-place turns slower than this, or turn in a wide arc instead of spinning on the spot, whenever the weapon is at full speed.
