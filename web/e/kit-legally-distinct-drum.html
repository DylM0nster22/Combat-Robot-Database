Legally Distinct — Open-Source 150g UK Drum Spinner Design

A free printable 150g UK-class antweight drum-spinner design by community builder Blessy (published on Thingiverse and documented on Hackaday.io), explicitly built as an homage/iteration on the established UK drum spinner 'Siren' — a clear, verified illustration of how the plastic scene iterates openly on successful designs.

Notes

Weight-classed here as 'fairyweight' per this database's vocabulary because it is a UK 150g class robot, NOT the US 1lb/454g plastic antweight this file is centered on — included because it's a well-documented, frequently cited example of the wider 'plastic ant' design-sharing culture, especially in the UK, where 'antweight' historically refers to the 150g bracket rather than 1lb. Concrete build details (verified via the Hackaday.io project page): drive uses 2x N20 gearmotors (~600rpm class), the drum weapon motor mount is sized for a BE1806 2300kv brushless motor with a 3mm bearing on the opposite side of the drum, and the design runs a 'malenki nano' micro ESC (a BBB Dual ESC was noted as a possible but tighter-fitting swap). The design iterated through weight: V2.1 was reported ~4g over the 150g limit, and V3.1 was revised to cut weight and reportedly finished at ~139g — a real example of the iterate-and-reprint weight-budget process that defines the plastic-ant/printable-ant design culture at any scale.
