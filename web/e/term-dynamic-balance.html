Dynamic balance

Balancing a rotating part in two separate planes along its length, not just its overall center of mass -- necessary for weapons with significant length along the rotation axis (long bars, wide drums) where the part can be statically balanced (center of mass on-axis) yet still wobble in operation because the mass distribution at one end differs from the other, creating a rocking couple. Checking this at the hobbyist level typically means balancing each end/plane of the weapon separately (e.g., weighing/trimming each half of a bar independently) rather than true multi-plane balancing-machine measurement.
