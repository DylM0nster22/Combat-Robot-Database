PLA+ (Modified PLA)

A modified PLA filament formulation marketed for improved impact resistance over standard PLA while keeping PLA's easy printability, one of the five materials SPARC and SCAR explicitly name as legal for plastic-ant chassis and weapons.

Materialplastic-antweightfilamentchassis-materiallegal-everywhere

Strengths

  • Easiest of the legal plastics to print reliably
  • Rarely warps, good for small precise parts
  • Explicitly legal in every plastic-ant ruleset reviewed

Weaknesses

  • Still more brittle than PETG/ABS under sharp impact despite the '+' marketing
  • Lower heat resistance than PETG/ABS
