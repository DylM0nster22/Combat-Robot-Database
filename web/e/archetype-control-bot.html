Control Bot

A weaponless robot built entirely around drivetrain and armour, winning on pushing, pinning, ring-outs and judges' decisions.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcontrolbeginnerreliability

Strengths

  • Every gram not spent on a weapon goes into being unkillable
  • Reliability wins tournaments — a bot that finishes every match accumulates decisions
  • Ideal first build: teaches driving, which matters more than weapons at this level

Weaknesses

  • Must win on judges' scores, which is never guaranteed
  • Against a damaging opponent you have to be perfect for three minutes

Notes

The control bot is the most underrated archetype for new builders. At 1 lb, more matches are lost to broken robots than to out-designed ones, and a robot that reliably drives for three minutes beats a fragile spinner more often than beginners expect.
