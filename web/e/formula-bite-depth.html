Bite depth from closing speed, weapon RPM, and tooth count

How far (in material depth) a spinning weapon's tooth advances into an opponent between successive tooth strikes, given how fast the two robots are closing together. Fewer teeth at the same RPM means a LONGER gap between hits, so each hit gets a deeper 'bite' -- the core reason single-tooth vertical spinners are favored for maximum per-hit penetration over multi-tooth designs.

Expression

f_hit = (rpm / 60) * tooth_count ; bite_depth = v_closing / f_hit

Variables

f_hithit frequency, hits/s
rpmweapon rotational speed, rpm
tooth_countnumber of teeth/striking points on the weapon
bite_depthmaterial depth engaged per hit, m
v_closingclosing speed between the two robots at the moment of contact, m/s

Worked example

Weapon at rpm = 20,000, single tooth (tooth_count = 1), closing speed v_closing = 1.5 m/s (combined approach speed of both robots). f_hit = (20000/60) * 1 = 333.3 hits/s. bite_depth = 1.5 / 333.3 = 0.0045 m (4.5 mm) engaged per hit.
