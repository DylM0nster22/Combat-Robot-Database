3D-Printed TPU Wheel

A wheel (tire, or tire+hub as one piece) 3D printed in flexible TPU filament (commonly 85-95A shore hardness), a zero-marginal-cost option extremely popular in plastic antweights since it fits the class's plastic-construction ethos and needs no purchased hub/tire hardware.

Componentplastic-antweightantweightwheel3d-printedtpubudgetplastic-antweight

Strengths

  • Essentially free if you already own a printer and filament
  • Can be printed as a single piece with the hub/shaft-bore built in, saving assembly and weight of a separate metal hub
  • Tread pattern, diameter, and width are all fully custom per design
  • Very impact-resistant, harder to crack than rigid printed wheels

Weaknesses

  • TPU can be harder to print reliably (stringing, needs a direct-drive extruder ideally) than rigid filaments
  • Grip is generally lower than a soft cast-silicone tire at similar hardness, though still much better than a rigid PLA/PETG wheel
  • Print layer lines/infill can affect roundness/balance if not printed carefully

Specifications

hardness shore a90

Notes

Common shore hardness for printable TPU filament is 85A-95A; softer (lower shore) TPUs exist but are harder to print and more prone to tearing on impact.
