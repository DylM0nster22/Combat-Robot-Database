Pololu Micro Metal Gearmotor, Extended Motor Shaft variant

A version of the micro metal gearmotor line with the motor's rear shaft extended through the back of the case, letting a builder mount an encoder disc, a second small pulley, or a cooling/inertia element on the motor's non-gearbox end without modifying the motor.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightdrive-motorbrushedpololumicro-metal-gearmotorextended-shaft

Strengths

  • Enables encoder feedback or a rear-mounted pulley/sprocket without custom machining
  • Same gearbox performance as the standard version

Weaknesses

  • Small weight/cost premium over the standard (non-extended) version
  • Rarely needed on a simple 2WD antweight without closed-loop control

Specifications

voltage nominal v6
shaft dia mm3

Notes

Useful mainly for control-bot builders who want wheel-speed encoders for closed-loop drive, or who need to power a secondary belt/pulley off the back of the motor in a 4WD belt-driven layout.
