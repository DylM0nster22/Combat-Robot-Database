AR500 Abrasion-Resistant Steel

Through-hardened abrasion-resistant steel plate (~500 Brinell) used as budget spinner armor and hammer/wedge faces where titanium's cost isn't justified.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightarmorsteelbudget

Strengths

  • Much cheaper per kg than titanium
  • Very hard, resists denting and gouging
  • Widely available as cut-to-size plate

Weaknesses

  • Roughly 1.8x the density of titanium for similar surface hardness — heavy for antweight budgets
  • Brittle at full hardness — can crack under a sharp point-load hit
  • Hard to fasten/drill without carbide or pre-drilled holes

Notes

AR500 makes sense in antweights mainly as small wear plates (weapon tips, wedge edges) rather than full armor shells, because its weight penalty versus titanium eats fast into a 1 lb budget. More common as a value pick in beetleweight and up.
