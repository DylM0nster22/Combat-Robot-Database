1306 brushless motor (weapon)

13mm x 6mm stator motor originally built for FPV micro racing quads (2-3 inch props); a very common plastic-ant and light 1 lb weapon motor because of its wide KV range and cheap availability.

Componentplastic-antweightfairyweightantweightweapon-motorsize-ladderoutrunnerracing-motor

Strengths

  • Huge range of KV options on the market (2200-4000kv) from racing-drone suppliers
  • 9x9mm M2 mount pattern is easy to adapt with a printed plate
  • Good torque for its 15-19g weight, popular for small vertical discs

Weaknesses

  • 3mm-class magnets can demagnetize if driven well past rated current during a hard stall
  • 1.5mm shaft is thin for anything beyond a small disc or paddle
