Captive Nut

A nut trapped in a printed pocket so a bolt can be tightened into it without access to the back.

Termantweightplastic-antweightfabrication

Notes

Cheaper than a heat-set insert and stronger than a tapped plastic thread. Design the pocket a few tenths oversize on the flats, add a small lead-in chamfer, and put a ceiling over it so the nut cannot fall out. The weakness is that the pocket walls can split under repeated tightening, so keep at least 2 mm of material around it.
