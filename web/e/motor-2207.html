2207 brushless motor (weapon)

22mm x 7mm stator, one of the most common freestyle/racing FPV motor sizes and a direct antweight favorite; Repeat Robotics' antweight hubmotor is built on this stator size for plug-and-play direct-drive weapons.

Componentantweightweapon-motorsize-ladderoutrunnerbread-and-butter

Strengths

  • Best stator-volume-to-weight ratio in the 22xx family, strong low-RPM torque
  • 5mm shaft handles direct-drive hub loads well
  • Base stator size used by purpose-built combat hubmotors (e.g. Repeat 2207)

Weaknesses

  • Near the top of what a 1 lb build can spend on motor weight alone if also running a full-size hub
