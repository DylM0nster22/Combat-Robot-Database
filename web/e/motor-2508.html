2508 brushless motor (weapon)

25mm x 8mm stator, a tall/high-torque long-range and heavy-lift drone motor; in combat it is a low-KV, high-torque option for belt-reduced 1 lb drums and bars that need bite more than raw tip speed.

Strengths

  • Very strong stall torque for its class, low KV keeps bite high even geared 1:1
  • Popular, well-supported size from long-range FPV so parts are cheap

Weaknesses

  • 75g+ is a big bite out of a 454g total robot weight; almost always needs the rest of the bot built light
