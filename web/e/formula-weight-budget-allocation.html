Weight budget allocation across subsystems

Splits the robot's total weight limit into subsystem budgets by percentage, the first step of any antweight design before a single part is chosen. Typical splits differ sharply between a metal-weapon spinner and a plastic-antweight build (see the two worked-walkthrough chunks).

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightweight-budgetdesign-process

Expression

weight_component_g = total_weight_g * (pct_component / 100)

Variables

weight_component_gweight budget for one subsystem, g
total_weight_gtotal robot weight limit, g (454 g for a 1 lb antweight)
pct_componentpercentage of total weight allocated to that subsystem

Worked example

A metal-weapon vertical-spinner budget at total_weight_g = 454 g: weapon 30% -> 4540.30 = 136.2 g; drive 20% -> 4540.20 = 90.8 g; armor/frame 30% -> 4540.30 = 136.2 g; electronics 12% -> 4540.12 = 54.5 g; fasteners/misc 8% -> 454*0.08 = 36.3 g. Sum = 136.2+90.8+136.2+54.5+36.3 = 454.0 g, exactly on budget.
