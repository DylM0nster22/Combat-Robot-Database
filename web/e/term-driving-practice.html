Driving Practice

Deliberate practice at driving, which decides more antweight matches than design does.

Termantweightplastic-antweightstrategy

Notes

The highest-value drills: driving a figure-eight without touching walls; driving the same course inverted; driving with one side disabled (unplug a motor); approaching a stationary target from an angle rather than head-on; and practising the first three seconds of a match over and over, because box rushes decide a surprising share of outcomes. Twenty minutes a week beats any component upgrade you can buy.
