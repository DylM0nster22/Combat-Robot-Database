NiMH Stick Pack (legacy antweight power)

Nickel-metal-hydride packs were the default antweight power source in the earliest days of the class (roughly the 1990s through mid-2000s, before LiPo became affordable and reliable), typically a 6-cell 7.2V stick pack. Almost entirely obsolete today because LiPo offers 2-3x the energy density and can supply far higher current for its weight.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightnimhlegacyobsolete

Strengths

  • Very tolerant of abuse and overdischarge compared to LiPo
  • No fire risk from puncture the way LiPo has
  • Simple to charge with a basic NiMH charger

Weaknesses

  • Roughly 1/3 the energy density of LiPo by weight, a major handicap at 1 lb
  • Cannot sustain the burst current a modern brushless spinner setup needs without severe sag
  • Self-discharges faster in storage than LiPo
