Stock Continuous-Rotation Micro Servo (FS90R-class)

A factory-built continuous-rotation micro servo (no user modification needed), functionally equivalent to a hand-modified 9g servo but sold ready to use, popular for beginner plastic antweight and fairyweight drive.

Componentplastic-antweightfairyweightdrive-motorservocontinuous-rotationbudgetbeginner

Strengths

  • Zero-modification, works out of the box
  • Same weight/cost/simplicity benefits as the hand-modified version, with more consistent "stopped" centering

Weaknesses

  • Same low speed/torque ceiling as any 9g-servo-based drive
  • Still uses a small plastic internal gearbox not designed for combat impacts

Specifications

weight g9
voltage nominal v5

Notes

A good first-build option for absolute beginners or very light plastic antweights where simplicity and low part count outweigh top-end performance.
