Tungsten Ballast (Heavy Alloy)

Very high-density tungsten or tungsten-heavy-alloy (90-97% W with Ni/Fe/Cu binder) blocks/putty used purely as ballast to hit a 1 lb weight limit exactly, or to shift center of mass/gyroscopic mass in a weapon.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightballastweight-tuninghigh-density

Strengths

  • Highest practical density available to hobbyists (~2.2x steel) — lets you add mass in the smallest possible volume
  • Non-toxic, dense alternative to lead for ballast
  • Great for loading spinner/drum rims to raise kinetic energy without changing outer diameter

Weaknesses

  • Expensive per kg/per gram compared to steel ballast
  • Small blocks are very heavy for their size — easy to blow a weight limit if not careful
  • Pure tungsten is brittle; heavy-alloy grades are tougher but still not structural armor

Notes

A common antweight trick is to press a few grams of tungsten cube or putty into a cavity to trim the last few grams up to the 1 lb (453.6g) limit, or to load the rim of a spinner drum/disc with tungsten inserts to raise its moment of inertia (and thus kinetic energy at a given RPM) without enlarging the weapon's diameter.
