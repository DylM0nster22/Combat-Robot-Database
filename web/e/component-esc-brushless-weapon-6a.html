Micro Brushless Weapon ESC (~6A)

A tiny brushless ESC (often an AM32 or BLHeli-based FPV-drone-derived board) used to run the weapon motor in a plastic ant, valued for its very low weight relative to the alternative of a brushed weapon motor and controller.

Componentplastic-antweightweaponescbrushless

Strengths

  • Extremely light
  • Programmable spin-up behavior (some support instant full throttle at arm for combat)

Weaknesses

  • Small package can be sensitive to heat if run continuously at max current
  • Needs correct motor-timing configuration for reliable spin-up under weapon load
