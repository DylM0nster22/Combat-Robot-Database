Polypropylene (PP) Filament

Very lightweight, chemically resistant, and extremely fatigue-resistant filament with a natural living-hinge quality — poor bed adhesion makes it hard to print, so it's used narrowly for flexible tabs/latches/hinges rather than armor.

Strengths

  • Lowest density of common printables — genuinely useful for a 1 lb weight budget
  • Outstanding fatigue life — can flex thousands of cycles as a living hinge without cracking
  • Excellent chemical resistance

Weaknesses

  • Warps and lifts off the bed badly without specific surface prep
  • Low stiffness/strength versus PLA or nylon
  • Rarely used for load-bearing armor because of poor print reliability and low rigidity

Notes

PP shows up in antweights almost exclusively as small flexible features — snap-fit battery doors, living-hinge latches, flexible tabs — leveraging its fatigue resistance rather than as a structural or armor material.
