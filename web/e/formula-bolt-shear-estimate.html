Bolt shear stress estimate

Estimates the shear stress carried by a group of bolts resisting a reaction force (e.g. a weapon-motor mount reacting the impact force), by spreading the force evenly across the bolts' shear cross-sectional area.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightstructureboltsfasteners

Expression

tau = F / (n_bolts * A_bolt)

Variables

taushear stress per bolt, Pa
Ftotal force the bolted joint must resist, N
n_boltsnumber of bolts sharing the load
A_boltcross-sectional (shear) area of one bolt, m^2 (= pi/4 * d^2)

Worked example

A weapon-motor mount uses n_bolts = 2 M3 bolts (d = 0.003 m). A_bolt = pi/4 0.003^2 = 0.7854 0.000009 = 7.065e-6 m^2. Reaction force F = 211.6 N (from the impact-force example). tau = 211.6 / (2 * 7.065e-6) = 211.6 / 1.413e-5 = 1.497e7 Pa = 15.0 MPa -- far below typical alloy-steel socket-head cap screw shear strength (~350-500 MPa), a safety factor of over 20x on a single hit, which is appropriate given bolts see this load repeatedly (fatigue) rather than just once.
