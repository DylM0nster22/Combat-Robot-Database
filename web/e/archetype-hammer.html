Hammer / Axe

A weighted head swung through an arc to concentrate its energy into a single point of impact.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightimpacthammersrimech

Strengths

  • Concentrated point impact can punch through thin top armour
  • Doubles as a self-righting mechanism
  • Scores well on aggression and looks dramatic

Weaknesses

  • Very low total energy compared to a spinner — the arm only accelerates over a short arc
  • Must be almost touching the opponent to land a hit
  • The arm and its pivot take the full reaction force

Notes

Hammers suffer from a simple energy problem: a spinner accumulates energy over seconds of spin-up, while a hammer only has the arc of its swing. At 1 lb the head mass you can afford is small enough that the result is rarely decisive. They remain popular because they self-right, look good, and are satisfying to drive.
