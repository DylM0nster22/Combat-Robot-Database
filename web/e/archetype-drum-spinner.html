Drum Spinner

A wide toothed cylinder spinning on a horizontal axis low at the front. Combines a vertical spinner's damage vector with exceptional bite.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightspinnerverticaldrumbite

Strengths

  • Best bite of any spinner: the large diameter and low mounting scoop under an opponent's edge
  • Wide face means you rarely miss laterally
  • Compact and rigid — the drum is its own structural member
  • Low gyroscopic effect compared to a large disc at the same energy

Weaknesses

  • Lower moment of inertia per gram than a bar, so less stored energy for the mass
  • Machining or printing a true, balanced drum is harder than cutting a disc
  • Dead-shaft drum construction is fiddly at 1 lb scale

Notes

Drums are arguably the best all-round antweight weapon: they hit upward like a vertical spinner but engage far more reliably because the drum body itself acts as a wedge that pulls the opponent in. The tradeoff is energy density — a drum's mass sits at a smaller radius than a bar's tips.

Weak against
