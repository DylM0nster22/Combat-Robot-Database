Horizontal Bar Spinner

A bar spinning on a vertical axis, sweeping a circle far wider than the robot. Hits from any direction in the horizontal plane.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightspinnerhorizontalreach

Strengths

  • Enormous reach — it threatens well outside the robot's footprint
  • Attacks the whole horizontal plane, so approach angle matters less
  • Devastating against other spinners' exposed weapons and against wheels

Weaknesses

  • Every hit throws the robot backwards as hard as the opponent — self-inflicted chaos
  • Loses to a low wedge that gets under before the bar can bite
  • Needs a lot of floor clearance discipline; scraping the floor kills energy

Notes

Horizontals have fallen out of top-level antweight favour because a wedge beats them reliably: the bar passes harmlessly over a well-built wedge while the wedge drives under. They remain excellent against other spinners and against anything with exposed wheels, and they are still the archetype most likely to end a match in one hit.
