Plastic Ant Wedge / Control Bot

A weaponless or lightly-weaponed robot built around a low front wedge and strong drivetrain, designed to win on driving skill, ring-out and judges' decision rather than weapon damage — often the recommended first build and a strong beginner-friendly counter to fragile plastic spinners.

Archetypeplastic-antweightcontrolwedgebeginnermetajudges-decision

Strengths

  • Nothing to break: no weapon motor, ESC, or fragile printed weapon to crack
  • Thick printed wedge is one of the best uses of plastic's weight-cheap bulk
  • Excellent way to learn driving and electronics before adding a weapon
  • Directly counters the fragile plastic-only spinner archetypes by denying them a clean hit

Weaknesses

  • Cannot win by knockout/damage, relies on ring-outs, pins and judges' decisions
  • Loses to a well-driven spinner that gets underneath or around the wedge

Notes

Almost every 'first plastic ant' guide recommends starting here because the failure mode (a cracked wedge) is a five-minute reprint, unlike a broken weapon motor mount.

Strong against

  • Plastic Ant Beater Bar / Egg-beater
