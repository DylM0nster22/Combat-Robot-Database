Silicone-Coated / Cast Silicone Tire

A foam or 3D-printed wheel core coated or wrapped in a thin layer of cast silicone rubber (Shore A ~20-50 durometer), a common DIY upgrade path when bare foam or bare plastic doesn't provide enough grip for a control bot or pushing match.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightwheelsiliconetractiondiy

Strengths

  • Significant traction improvement over bare foam or bare plastic/TPU
  • Durometer is tunable by the builder (softer = more grip but faster wear, harder = more durable but less grip)
  • Can be applied over an existing lightweight foam or printed core, keeping most of the weight savings

Weaknesses

  • Requires casting/mixing 2-part silicone rubber (extra build step, cure time, some skill)
  • Adds some weight over bare foam
  • Tackier compounds pick up arena debris faster, sometimes needing cleaning mid-event

Specifications

hardness shore a30

Notes

Shore A 30-50 durometer silicone/urethane compounds are commonly cited as delivering the best grip on polished wood or acrylic arena floors without being so soft they wear out quickly or gum up with debris.
