Mechanical power delivered at a given speed

Basic power = force x velocity relationship, used to check how much continuous power the drivetrain is actually asking of the battery/ESC at cruising speed versus during a hard push.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightdrivetrainpower

Expression

P = F * v

Variables

Pmechanical power delivered, W
Fforce being delivered (driving or pushing force), N
vspeed at which that force is being delivered, m/s

Worked example

Cruising at top speed v_top = 0.921 m/s while only overcoming rolling resistance F_roll = 0.223 N (about 5% of weight): P = 0.223 0.921 = 0.205 W -- trivial power draw at cruise. Pushing hard at the traction limit F_max = 3.56 N while still moving at 0.921 m/s: P = 3.56 0.921 = 3.28 W -- the practical upper bound on continuous drivetrain mechanical power for this robot.
