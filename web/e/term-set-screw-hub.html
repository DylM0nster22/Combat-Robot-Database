Set-screw hub

The simplest and lightest weapon-to-shaft attachment: a round bore with one or more screws (often against a flat ground into the shaft) that clamp the hub on by friction and slight shaft deformation. Cheap, adjustable, and easy to remove, but it is a friction joint -- under the shock torque of a hard weapon impact it can slip on the shaft, throwing the weapon's timing/balance off or losing drive entirely, which is why harder-hitting antweight builds often upgrade to a keyway or broached hex once set-screw slip becomes a recurring failure.
