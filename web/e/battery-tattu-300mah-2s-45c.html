Tattu 300mAh 2S 45C LiPo

A very common micro LiPo pack used in antweights and micro FPV, sized to fit inside the tightest 1 lb chassis while still driving a small brushless weapon and a pair of brushed drive motors.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightlipo2sbudgetsmall-format

Strengths

  • Widely stocked by FPV/combat vendors
  • Good size-to-power ratio for micro antweights
  • Burst rating (90C) covers weapon spin-up spikes

Weaknesses

  • 45C continuous is optimistic on paper; real sustained current is lower
  • 300mAh is tight for a 3-minute match with a hungry spinner

Notes

Tattu (made by Grepow) is one of the most common battery brands cross-used from FPV drones into antweight combat because the physical sizes and JST/PH-style connectors already match what small combat electronics expect. This exact SKU is also sold in a 75C LiHV version (see battery-tattu-300mah-2s-75c-hv).
