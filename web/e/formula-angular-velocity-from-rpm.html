Angular velocity from RPM

Converts a motor or weapon speed given in RPM (revolutions per minute) into angular velocity in radians per second, the unit every rotational-KE and torque formula on this page needs.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightrotationalconversioncore-formula

Expression

omega = rpm * 2 * pi / 60

Variables

omegaangular velocity, rad/s
rpmrotational speed, revolutions per minute

Worked example

A vertical-spinner disc is spinning at 20,000 rpm. omega = 20000 2 3.14159 / 60 = 20000 * 6.28318 / 60 = 125,663.7 / 60 = 2094.4 rad/s.
