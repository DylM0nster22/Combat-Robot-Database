Generic N30 Gearmotor, 6V, 50:1

The larger sibling of the N20, using a bigger-diameter motor can (~30mm class) for more torque and current handling at the cost of extra weight; used in antweights (not usually plastic antweights, where every gram counts more) when N20 torque isn't enough but a 16mm FingerTech-class motor is still preferred to save weight.

Strengths

  • More torque and current headroom than N20 for a modest weight increase
  • Still cheap relative to name-brand combat motors

Weaknesses

  • Same quality-lottery sourcing risk as other generic N-series motors
  • Larger diameter can be harder to package in a slim 1 lb chassis than a 16mm motor
