Nylon (Printed)

A tough, wear-resistant, semi-flexible printable nylon filament that, like TPU, sits outside the base SPARC 4.4.1 approved list and is explicitly banned by name in SCAR's plastic ant FAQ, despite being a popular choice for gears and living-hinge parts in general 3D-printing use.

Strengths

  • Very tough and abrasion-resistant in general use

Weaknesses

  • Explicitly banned by SCAR's plastic ant FAQ
  • Not on the SPARC baseline approved list either
  • Moisture-sensitive, harder to print reliably than PLA/PETG/ABS
