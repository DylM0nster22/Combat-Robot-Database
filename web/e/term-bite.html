Bite (weapon engagement depth)

How deeply a spinning weapon's tooth/edge engages an opponent's armor per revolution before sliding off -- determined by weapon RPM relative to the combined driving speed of both robots, not by tip speed or KE alone. Lower weapon RPM (for the same tip speed, i.e. bigger diameter/lower KV) generally gives more bite because the tooth has more time to dig in per revolution.

Notes

This is why the community rule of thumb is 'low KV + high cell count beats high KV + low cells' for the same top speed: a bigger, slower-spinning weapon captures more material per hit even though the tip speed and stored energy can be identical to a smaller, faster one.
