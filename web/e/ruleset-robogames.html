RoboGames Combat Robot Rules

RoboGames (formerly ROBOlympics) is a large annual US multi-discipline robotics expo whose combat division runs six standard weight classes (150g through 250lb) plus parallel walking-bonus classes at 100% extra weight and separate 1lb/3lb autonomous divisions. It has one of the most detailed public banned-weapon and judging-scoring rulesets of any hobby organization.
