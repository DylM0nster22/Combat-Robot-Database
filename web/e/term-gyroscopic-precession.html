Gyroscopic precession

The tendency of a spinning object's rotation axis to react to an applied torque by tilting perpendicular to both the spin axis and the applied torque, rather than in the direction naively expected. In vertical spinners, turning the chassis while the weapon is spinning applies exactly this kind of torque, producing an unwanted tipping reaction.
