FingerTech Twist Hub

An anodized 6061 aluminum hub secured to the motor shaft with an 8-32 setscrew, holding the foam tire tightly via a locking washer; sold in Thin (0.50in) and Wide (0.75in) widths for both 3mm and 4mm shafts.

Componentantweightplastic-antweighthubfingertechaluminum

Strengths

  • Setscrew retention onto the motor shaft is very secure against slipping under torque
  • Aluminum build is strong and light
  • Available for both 3mm and 4mm shafts, and Thin/Wide widths

Weaknesses

  • Heavier than the plastic Lite Hub
  • Setscrew must be properly torqued/thread-locked or it can back out over repeated hits
