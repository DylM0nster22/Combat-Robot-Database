Cast Polyurethane Elastomer

Cast or machined polyurethane rubber in a range of durometers — tougher and more abrasion/tear resistant than silicone, used for shock-absorbing bumpers, wheel tires, and flexible wedge lips.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightelastomershock-absorptionwheelscompliance

Strengths

  • Much better abrasion and tear resistance than silicone at similar softness
  • Wide range of durometers available to tune grip vs durability
  • Good rebound/energy return for wheels

Weaknesses

  • Lower heat tolerance than silicone
  • Not structural — purely a compliant/grip material

Notes

Polyurethane's durometer selection is a real engineering choice: softer PU wheel tires give more traction but wear faster and can deform under a weapon motor's torque, while harder PU gives more durability at some traction cost — the same softness-vs-durability tradeoff applies to PU wedge lips and bumper pads used for deliberate compliance against weapon impacts.
