Effective (reduced) mass of a spinning weapon at its tooth

Converts a rotating weapon's inertia into an equivalent straight-line mass, as felt at one specific radius (usually the tooth/tip). This lets you treat a weapon hit like a simple linear collision: KE = 0.5 * m_eff * v_tip^2 gives the same energy as the rotational formula.

Expression

m_eff = I / r_tip^2

Variables

m_effeffective linear mass at the reference radius, kg
Itotal weapon moment of inertia about its spin axis, kg*m^2
r_tipradius from the spin axis to the reference point (usually the tooth tip), m

Worked example

I_total = 3.25e-5 kgm^2, r_tip = 0.032 m. m_eff = 3.25e-5 / 0.032^2 = 3.25e-5 / 0.001024 = 0.03174 kg (31.7 g). Check: KE = 0.5 0.03174 67.0^2 = 0.5 0.03174 * 4489 = 71.2 J, matching formula-rotational-ke's 71.3 J.
