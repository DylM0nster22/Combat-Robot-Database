Technical Inspection (Plastic-Only Check)

The pre-event safety/legality check where an event official visually inspects a plastic ant for compliance: verifying the chassis/armor/weapon are printed plastic, checking that any metal fasteners are countersunk/shrouded rather than proud, and looking for banned materials like TPU, nylon, or composite reinforcement layered into a printed part.

Termplastic-antweightrulesinspectionenforcement

Notes

Per SCAR's FAQ, inspection is primarily visual and datasheets are not required by default, but 'suspicious' builds (e.g. an implausibly stiff or dense-looking part) can trigger further investigation, sometimes including a request to cut into or weigh/measure a suspect part.
