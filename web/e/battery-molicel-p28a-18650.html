Molicel INR18650-P28A (Li-ion, context)

A high-drain 18650 cylindrical Li-ion cell, used here as the reference point for Li-ion in combat robotics: much higher capacity-per-cell and better cycle life than a LiPo pouch cell, but lower cell voltage (3.6-3.7V nominal vs 3.7V LiPo, similar) and a hard steel/nickel can that adds weight relative to energy density, plus a lower safe continuous discharge current per gram than a hot LiPo pouch.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightli-ion18650context

Strengths

  • Very high energy density per dollar and excellent cycle life vs LiPo
  • Rigid metal can survives rough handling better than a pouch cell
  • Popular and well-proven in flashlights, vapes, and e-bikes so cells are cheap and easy to source

Weaknesses

  • A single 18650 weighs ~48g for 2800mAh — far too heavy per Wh for a 1 lb robot once you need series cells for voltage
  • 35A max discharge on one cell is high in absolute terms but low relative to the weight, and nowhere near what a spinner needs at 3S-4S-equivalent voltage from series 18650s
  • Round cells are hard to package efficiently in a flat antweight chassis

Notes

18650/21700 Li-ion (Molicel, Samsung, LG, Sony/Murata) is essentially never used as the main weapon+drive pack in a competitive 1 lb antweight because the form factor and weight-per-amp are wrong for the class; it shows up mostly as context, in bigger weight classes (beetleweight and up) or in stationary test rigs, not on 1 lb bots.
