Foam Padding (EVA / Closed-Cell PU Foam)

Lightweight closed-cell foam used as internal padding around batteries, receivers, and PCBs — deliberately compliant, sacrificial cushioning that prevents shock-induced solder joint failure or battery puncture without adding meaningful weight.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightelastomerfoaminternal-paddingshock-protection

Strengths

  • Extremely lightweight — negligible weight-budget cost
  • Cheap and easy to cut/fit into odd internal cavities
  • Effective at damping high-frequency shock transmitted into electronics

Weaknesses

  • Not structural in any sense
  • Can compress permanently over many hits and lose cushioning effectiveness
  • Flammable — keep away from LiPo failure sites

Notes

A few grams of foam wrapped around a battery or wedged behind a flight controller is a deliberate design choice: rigid mounting transmits shock impulses directly into solder joints and battery cells, which is a common cause of in-match electrical failure even when the armor itself survives a hit; foam sacrifices a little internal volume to break that direct shock path.
