Generic N20 Gearmotor, 12V, 1000:1

The slowest, highest-torque stock N20 ratio; the 1000:1 (and 380:1) versions get a slightly longer, reinforced gearbox and a higher torque rating than the rest of the line, making them the ones most likely to survive at this extreme reduction.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightdrive-motorbrushedn-seriesbudgethigh-torque

Strengths

  • Reinforced gearbox stage rated to ~25 kgmm (2.5 kg-cm), higher than the ~20 kgmm (2.0 kg-cm) rating of lower ratios
  • Very high torque for actuators/lifters

Weaknesses

  • Far too slow for drive; this ratio belongs on weapon-lift or self-righting mechanisms, not wheels
  • Gearbox is 3.5mm longer than other ratios, a minor packaging difference
