Crusher

A hardened beak or spike driven by a very high-force, low-speed actuator to punch through an opponent's armour.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcrusheradvancednovelty

Strengths

  • Does damage no spinner can do — it pierces rather than dents
  • Holding an opponent pierced is total control
  • Immune to the energy-transfer problem that limits spinners

Weaknesses

  • Extremely slow; you must already have the opponent held still
  • Hard to generate meaningful force in 454 g
  • Very complex for the result

Notes

Crushers scale badly downward. The force needed to pierce even thin armour requires gearing that makes the mechanism slow and heavy, and a 1 lb robot has neither the weight budget nor the traction to hold an opponent still while it works. They appear at antweight mainly as novelty or demonstration builds.
