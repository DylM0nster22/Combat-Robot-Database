Stall current from voltage and winding resistance

The current a motor draws when its rotor is locked (RPM = 0), limited only by the winding resistance since there is no back-EMF at zero speed. This is the highest current the motor (and its ESC) will ever be asked to pass.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightelectricalstall-currentresistance

Expression

I_stall = V / R

Variables

I_stallstall current, A
Vapplied voltage, V
Rmotor winding resistance (phase-to-phase or armature), ohm

Worked example

A small brushed antweight drive motor has R = 1.2 ohm and is run at V = 7.4 V (2S): I_stall = 7.4 / 1.2 = 6.17 A.
