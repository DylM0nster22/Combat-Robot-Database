Moment of inertia — thin ring / hoop (all mass at one radius)

MOI of a thin hoop or ring where essentially all the mass sits at a single radius, such as a steel reinforcement band shrunk onto the rim of a disc weapon.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightrotationalmoiweapon-inertia

Expression

I = m * r^2

Variables

Imoment of inertia, kg*m^2
mmass of the ring, kg
rradius of the ring, m

Worked example

A thin steel rim-reinforcement ring has mass m = 0.015 kg (15 g) at radius r = 0.030 m. I = 0.015 0.030^2 = 0.015 0.0009 = 0.0000135 kgm^2 (1.35e-5 kgm^2).
