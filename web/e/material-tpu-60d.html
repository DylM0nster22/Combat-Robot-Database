TPU 60D (Rigid Thermoplastic Polyurethane)

The stiffest common TPU grade, rated on the Shore D scale (roughly equivalent to a very firm ~95-98A) — bridges the gap between fully rigid filaments and soft Shore-A TPUs, giving some compliance while still holding structural shape.

Materialantweightplastic-antweight3d-printedtpusemi-rigidhybrid-armor

Strengths

  • Retains some structural rigidity while still flexing under sharp impacts instead of cracking
  • Easier to use in load-bearing-adjacent parts than softer TPUs
  • Good middle ground for parts that need both durability and some shape retention

Weaknesses

  • Absorbs less impact energy than 85A/95A because it deforms less
  • Still far more flexible than PLA/PETG/nylon — not a substitute for a rigid frame
  • Less commonly stocked than 95A, so pricier/harder to source in some regions

Notes

TPU60D is the choice when a builder wants a part that behaves mostly like a tough rigid plastic but shouldn't shatter under a glancing hit — wedge skirts and hinge components that see both structural load and impact are the classic use case.
