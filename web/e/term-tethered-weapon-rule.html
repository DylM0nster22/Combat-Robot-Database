Tethered Detachable Weapon Rule

A safety rule requiring that any weapon part designed to detach in combat (e.g. a thrown hammer head or a shattering disc meant to break away) remain physically tethered to the main robot body, with a maximum tether length, to prevent uncontrolled projectiles reaching the crowd or arena walls.

Notes

AWS (UK) caps detachable-weapon tethers at 3 feet (914mm) and caps pressurized weapon systems at 100 psi (7 bar).
