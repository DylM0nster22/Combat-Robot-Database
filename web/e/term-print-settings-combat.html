Combat Print Settings

The print profile changes that make a printed part survive combat.

Termantweightplastic-antweightprinting

Specifications

perimeters5
infill pct25
nozzle mm0.6

Notes

Relative to a normal profile: raise nozzle temperature toward the top of the filament's range (better layer bonding), reduce part cooling for PLA+ and PETG (cooling fast makes weak layer bonds, though too little causes drooping), slow the print down (more time at temperature at the layer interface), increase perimeters to 4-6, use a 0.6 mm nozzle, and raise extrusion width slightly. Expect a worse surface finish and a much stronger part.
