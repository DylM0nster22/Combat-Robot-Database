Melty Brain

The entire robot spins continuously and translates by pulsing its drive in sync with rotation, using an accelerometer to know which way is forward.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightspinnersoftwareadvanced

Strengths

  • No weapon motor needed — the drivetrain is the weapon
  • Omnidirectional threat like a shell spinner, with far simpler mechanics
  • Weapon energy and mobility come from the same system

Weaknesses

  • Requires custom firmware and an accelerometer; this is a software project as much as a mechanical one
  • Translation is slow and imprecise compared to normal driving
  • Loses all weapon energy the moment it needs to manoeuvre
  • Hard to drive and hard for judges to score

Notes

Melty brain — also called translational drift — is the most software-dependent archetype in combat robotics. The controller reads centripetal acceleration to estimate heading, then pulses each drive motor once per revolution so net thrust points in the commanded direction. It is a brilliant solution to getting spinner energy without a weapon motor, and a difficult one to make reliable.
