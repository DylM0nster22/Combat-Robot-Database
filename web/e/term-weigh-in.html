Weigh-In

The check that your robot is inside the class weight limit.

Termantweightplastic-antweightstrategy

Notes

Antweight is 454 g. Weigh on your own scale before you travel and again at the event, because event scales are the ones that count and they disagree with yours. Build to about 445 g so you have margin for threadlocker, tape, and the bolt you add in the pits. Being overweight at check-in is one of the commonest ways to lose a match without fighting.
