4140 Chromoly Steel

A common medium-carbon chromoly alloy steel used for antweight weapon bodies, bars, and shafts where a good combination of strength, toughness, and machinability is needed and full through-hardening of the whole part is not required.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightweapon-steelspinner-barbeater-barheat-treatmaterialsteelshaft

Strengths

  • Cheap and everywhere as pre-hardened (~28-32 HRC) round/flat bar — no heat treat oven needed
  • Can be heat treated further (quench and temper) up to ~50-55 HRC for harder weapon edges
  • Good toughness-to-cost ratio for beater bars

Weaknesses

  • No nickel content reduces fracture toughness versus 4340 at high hardness
  • Lower peak toughness than S7 at equivalent hardness — more prone to chipping in hard spinner-vs-spinner hits

Notes

For 4140 beater bars a hardness around HRC 50 is commonly cited as the sweet spot balancing edge bite against chipping resistance; store-bought 4140 pre-hard round bar (~28-32 HRC) is popular for horizontal spinner bars and hammer arms precisely because it needs zero heat-treat step and is tough enough for most antweight impacts as-is.
