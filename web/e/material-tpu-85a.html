TPU 85A (Soft Thermoplastic Polyurethane)

Softer, more compliant Shore-A TPU grade than 95A — absorbs impact even more readily by deforming further, at the cost of being harder to print (needs direct-drive, slow speeds) and offering less rebound stiffness.

Materialantweightplastic-antweight3d-printedtpuflexible-armorsoft

Strengths

  • Even better energy absorption/compliance than 95A — compresses more readily under impact
  • Very high elongation-to-break, essentially immune to cracking

Weaknesses

  • Harder to print reliably than 95A — needs direct drive and careful tuning
  • Softer means less rebound/structural contribution — purely a compliant skin, cannot contribute any rigidity
  • Can feel "mushy"/under-supported if used over too thin a rigid backer

Notes

85A is chosen over 95A when a builder wants maximum shock absorption (e.g., a very exposed leading edge) and is willing to accept the harder print process; it's less popular than 95A specifically because many hobby printers with Bowden extruders struggle to feed it consistently.
