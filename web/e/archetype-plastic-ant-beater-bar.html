Plastic Ant Beater Bar / Egg-beater

A low, wide horizontal weapon bar mounted close to the floor at the front of the robot that beats upward into an opponent's underside, similar in geometry to a drum spinner but usually a simpler bar rather than a full cylinder, printed thick to survive repeated low-clearance strikes.

Strengths

  • Cheaper/simpler to print than a full drum
  • Low profile discourages opposing wedges

Weaknesses

  • Narrower than a drum so it can miss off-center hits
  • Bar ends are a stress concentration point that tends to crack first
