Moment of inertia — solid disc / cylinder about its central axis

MOI of a uniform solid disc (or short solid cylinder) spinning about the axis through its own center, perpendicular to its face. This is the base case for most vertical-spinner and drum weapon discs.

Expression

I = 0.5 * m * r^2

Worked example
