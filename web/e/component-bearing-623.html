623 bearing (3x10x4mm)

A very small deep-groove ball bearing, 3mm bore x 10mm OD x 4mm width, used on the smallest antweight and plastic-antweight weapon and drive shafts where every gram counts.

Componentantweightplastic-antweightbearinghardware

Strengths

  • extremely light and compact
  • cheap and widely stocked

Weaknesses

  • very low shock/radial load capacity -- easily destroyed by a hard direct impact
  • small bore limits shaft diameter and therefore bending stiffness/strength
