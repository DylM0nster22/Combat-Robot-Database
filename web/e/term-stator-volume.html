Stator volume (diameter x height)

The product of a brushless motor's stator diameter and stator height/stack length, encoded directly in FPV motor names (e.g. '2207' = 22mm diameter x 7mm height). Stator volume is the single best predictor of how much torque and continuous power a motor can produce, because it sets how much copper and iron can be packed in to carry current and hold magnetic flux without saturating.

Notes

Diameter mostly buys torque (longer moment arm for the magnetic force acting at the rotor's radius); height mostly buys sustained power/RPM headroom (more copper per phase, more heat capacity, less current needed for the same torque). Two motors with the same stator volume but different diameter/height splits behave differently: a wide-short stator (e.g. 2504) favors low-RPM torque, a narrow-tall stator (e.g. 1408) favors higher RPM at less bulk. For weapons, bigger stator volume at a given KV always means more available stall torque and more thermal mass to survive hard stalls.
