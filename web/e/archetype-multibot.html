Multibot / Swarm

The class weight limit divided between two or more robots that fight as a team.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightmultibotstrategy

Strengths

  • Two robots can pincer an opponent from both sides at once
  • Losing one robot does not necessarily lose the match
  • Forces the opponent to split attention

Weaknesses

  • Each robot is tiny and individually fragile
  • Usually both must be immobilised to lose, but check your ruleset — some require only one
  • Driving two robots on one transmitter is genuinely hard

Notes

Multibots split 454 g into pieces that are each easy to destroy, in exchange for numbers and positioning. Rules vary sharply on what counts as a knockout, so read the specific event's ruleset before committing to the strategy.
