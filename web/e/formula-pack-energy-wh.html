Pack Energy (Watt-hours)

Converts a pack's mAh capacity and nominal voltage into total stored energy in watt-hours, the unit that lets you compare packs of different cell counts and capacities on equal footing.

Formulaenergywatt-hourssizing

Expression

Energy_Wh = (Capacity_mAh / 1000) * Voltage_nominal_V

Variables

Energy_Whtotal stored energy in the pack, watt-hours
Capacity_mAhrated capacity in milliamp-hours
Voltage_nominal_Vnominal pack voltage (3.7V/cell LiPo, 3.8V/cell LiHV, times cell count)

Worked example

A Tattu 300mAh 2S pack: 7.4V nominal. Energy = (300/1000) 7.4 = 2.22 Wh. A CNHL LiHV MiniStar 450mAh 3S pack at 11.4V nominal: (450/1000) 11.4 = 5.13 Wh, versus the standard-LiPo 450mAh 3S sibling at 11.1V nominal: (450/1000) * 11.1 = 5.0 Wh — about 2.7% more usable energy from LiHV at the same mAh rating, and more once you account for LiHV's higher voltage across the whole discharge curve, not just at nominal.
