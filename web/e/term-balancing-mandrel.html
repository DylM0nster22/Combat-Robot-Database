Balancing mandrel / balancing stand

A simple hobbyist static-balancing setup: the weapon is mounted on a precision shaft (mandrel) or its own shaft, which is then rested on a pair of level, low-friction supports -- hardened knife edges, a set of unloaded bearings, or even two parallel round rods. Gravity rotates the assembly until the heavy side settles at the bottom; that spot is marked, and material is removed there (or added to the opposite side) and the check repeated until the weapon stays put in any rotational position, indicating the center of mass is on the shaft axis.
