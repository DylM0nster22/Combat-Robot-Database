PETG

A tougher, more impact-ductile filament than PLA, widely considered the best default choice for plastic-ant weapon discs/drums and load-bearing chassis members because it bends and absorbs energy rather than shattering.

Materialplastic-antweightfilamentweapon-materialchassis-materiallegal-everywhere

Strengths

  • Best combination of legal-everywhere status and real-world impact toughness
  • More heat resistant than PLA (useful near weapon-motor heat)
  • Ductile failure mode (bends/cracks slowly) instead of shattering

Weaknesses

  • More prone to stringing/oozing than PLA, needs tuned retraction settings
  • Slightly heavier per volume than PLA/ABS
