Polycarbonate (PC) Filament

High-strength, high-temperature 3D print filament — strongest common FDM engineering filament by some tensile/impact metrics, but demanding to print (very high temperatures, enclosure required) and less commonly used than PETG/nylon in casual antweight builds.

Strengths

  • High strength and heat resistance among printables
  • Good impact toughness when printed well
  • Can approach injection-molded PC sheet toughness with well-tuned settings

Weaknesses

  • Difficult to print — needs a high-temp hotend, heated enclosure, and dry filament (also somewhat hygroscopic)
  • More expensive than PLA/PETG/ABS
  • Overkill/impractical for many antweight budgets given the printer requirements

Notes

PC filament is a premium, higher-effort option mostly used by builders with enclosed high-temp printers chasing maximum structural strength — it's less common in the plastic-antweight scene than PLA/PETG/ABS simply because the printer requirements exclude a lot of hobby-grade machines.
