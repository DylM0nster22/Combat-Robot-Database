Edge preparation (static weapons)

The sharpening angle, edge geometry, and heat treatment applied to a fork, spike, or wedge's leading edge. A thin, low included angle bites into opponent material more easily but chips or rolls over on a hard impact; a thicker, more obtuse angle survives repeated hard hits better but requires more closing force/speed to penetrate at all. Hardening the edge (case hardening a mild-steel wedge lip, or using a pre-hardened tool steel insert) lets a bot keep a thinner, more aggressive angle without it deforming on contact.
