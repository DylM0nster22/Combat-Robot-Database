KV (motor velocity constant)

The unloaded RPM a brushless motor spins per volt applied, in RPM/V. Determines top speed for a given voltage, but says nothing about torque or power by itself -- a motor's stator size and winding resistance matter just as much.

Termglossarykvmotor-electrical

Notes

KV is fixed by the motor's winding turns/wire gauge, not by voltage or load. Doubling voltage roughly doubles no-load RPM for the same KV motor; changing KV via rewinding changes the RPM-per-volt but trades off torque per amp inversely (lower KV windings of the same stator generally deliver more torque per amp, higher KV windings more RPM per amp).
