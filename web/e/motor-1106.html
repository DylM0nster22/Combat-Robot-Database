1106 brushless motor (weapon)

11mm x 6mm stator motor, one step up from 1104 in stator height/torque, used for slightly heavier plastic-ant vertical spinners and paddle weapons.

Strengths

  • Noticeably more torque than 1104 for only ~2g extra weight
  • Still cheap, widely available as whoop replacement motors

Weaknesses

  • Still a whoop-grade bearing/shaft; not shock-rated for weapon impacts
  • Small mounting screws (M2) strip easily
