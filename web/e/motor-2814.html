2814 brushless motor (weapon)

28mm x 14mm stator, the classic heavy-lift/heavy-ant-beetle-crossover size; the Cobra 2814 in particular is a well-known belt-drive weapon motor used across heavy 1 lb and light beetleweight spinners.

Componentbeetleweightweapon-motorsize-ladderoutrunnerheavy-ant-crossoverbelt-drive

Strengths

  • Huge stator volume gives serious torque and thermal headroom for belt-driven drums/discs
  • Cobra 2814 1850kv specifically documented as a belt-drive combat weapon motor choice

Weaknesses

  • Well over 1 lb (454g) motor-only weight budget on its own in some windings; realistically a beetleweight part used at the antweight/beetle crossover on the lightest windings
