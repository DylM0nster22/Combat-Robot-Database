Self-Righting

Getting back onto your wheels after being flipped.

Termantweightplastic-antweightdesignreliability

Notes

Three approaches: be invertible so it does not matter, use the weapon's reaction torque to flip yourself, or carry a dedicated mechanism. At 1 lb, invertibility is almost always the right answer because a srimech costs grams the weight budget cannot spare.
