Required motor KV from target tip speed and battery voltage

Back-solves the motor KV (or KV x gear-ratio) needed to hit a target tip speed on a given battery voltage, the single most common weapon-motor sizing question in the hobby.

Formulaformulakv-selection

Expression

RPM_target = v_mph * 336.14 / D_in   |   KV_needed = (RPM_target * ratio) / V_pack

Variables

v_mphtarget tip speed, miles/hour
D_inweapon diameter, inches
RPM_targetweapon RPM needed to hit that tip speed
ratioreduction ratio, weapon-pulley-teeth / motor-pulley-teeth (1.0 for direct drive)
V_packfully-charged pack voltage, e.g. 12.6V for 3S, 16.8V for 4S
KV_neededmotor KV to select, in RPM per volt

Worked example

Target: 250 mph tip speed on a 4 in disc, direct drive, 3S (12.6V full charge). RPM_target = 250 336.14 / 4 = 21,009 RPM. KV_needed = 21009 1.0 / 12.6 = 1667 kv. For an 8 in bar at the same 250 mph target, RPM_target halves to ~10,500 RPM, so KV_needed also halves to ~833 kv -- illustrating why bigger weapons want lower KV for the same tip speed.
