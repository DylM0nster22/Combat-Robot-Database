Storage Voltage

The recommended per-cell voltage (about 3.8V/cell, roughly 3.7-3.85V) at which a LiPo/LiHV pack should be kept for any period longer than a few days, chosen to minimize chemical stress on the cell compared to storing it fully charged or fully depleted.
