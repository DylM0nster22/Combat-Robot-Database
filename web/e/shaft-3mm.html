3 mm weapon shaft

The smallest practical weapon shaft for a 1 lb robot, usually hardened steel.

Componentantweightplastic-antweight

Strengths

  • Light
  • Fits 623 bearings, which are tiny and cheap

Weaknesses

  • Bends under real impact loads
  • Very little material to cut a flat or a keyway into

Specifications

dia mm3
typical materialhardened steel or drill rod

Notes

A 3 mm shaft is fine for a small, low-energy weapon and marginal for anything else. Bending is the failure mode, and a bent shaft immediately becomes an unbalanced weapon that destroys its own bearings. If your weapon is over about 100 J, go to 4 or 5 mm.
