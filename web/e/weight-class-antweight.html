Antweight (1 lb)

The classic North American entry class, capped at 1 lb (454 g). It is the most-run class at US and Canadian events (SPARC, RoboGames, Motorama, Robot Ruckus, Bot Blast) and is roughly three times heavier than the UK 'Antweight' (150 g), a naming collision that trips up new builders reading rules from the wrong region.

Notes

Because it is 3x the mass of the UK fairyweight/antweight, US 1lb antweights typically run larger motors, bigger batteries, and heavier armor than their UK 150g counterparts even though both are called 'antweight'.
