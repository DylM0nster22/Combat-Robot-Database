Printed Hub + TPU Tire Wheel

A 3D-printed rigid hub (PLA+/PETG) with a separately printed TPU tire pressed or glued on, the standard plastic-ant wheel: fully print-and-replace, and legal everywhere since both hub and tire are on the approved plastic list (subject to local TPU rulings).

Componentplastic-antweightwheeldrivetpuprinted

Strengths

  • Essentially free once you own a printer and TPU spool
  • Easy to redesign tread pattern for grip
  • Print-and-replace after wear or damage

Weaknesses

  • TPU is explicitly banned by some events (e.g. SCAR); always check local rules before relying on it
  • Lower grip than purpose-made silicone/foam tires unless tread is well designed
