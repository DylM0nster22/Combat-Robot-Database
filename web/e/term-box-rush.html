Box Rush

Charging the opponent the instant the match starts, before their weapon reaches speed.

Termantweightplastic-antweightdrivingstrategy

Notes

The standard counter to any spinner with a long spin-up. To defend it, spin up before the count where rules allow, start at an angle rather than square on, and build a robot whose front is safe to be hit at any weapon speed. Driving away from a box rush usually works better than trying to meet it head-on.
