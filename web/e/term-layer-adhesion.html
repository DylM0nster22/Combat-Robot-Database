Layer Adhesion

How strongly consecutive printed layers bond — the dominant weakness of any FDM part.

Termantweightplastic-antweightprintingmaterials

Specifications

cross layer strength pct50

Notes

An FDM part is anisotropic. Along the layers it approaches the bulk strength of the plastic; across the layers it is typically around half that, because each layer bonds only by partial remelting of the one below. Every design decision in a printed combat part follows from this: orient so impacts load the layers in shear or compression rather than pulling them apart, print hotter and slower to improve the bond, and never place a layer boundary across the path of a known load.
