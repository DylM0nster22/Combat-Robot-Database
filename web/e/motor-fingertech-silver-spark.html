FingerTech "Silver Spark" 16mm Gearmotor

The default drive gearmotor for 1 lb antweight robots: a Mabuchi FK-050 brushed motor mated to an all-metal 16mm spur gearhead, sold across a ladder of 9 gear ratios from 11.1:1 (fast) to 600:1 (slow/high-torque).

Componentantweightplastic-antweightdrive-motorbrushed16mmfingertechworkhorse

Strengths

  • All-metal gearbox is far more durable than plastic-geared alternatives
  • 9 stock ratios cover the whole speed/torque range antweights need
  • Extra-long 3mm shaft allows direct (live-axle) wheel mounting
  • Very well documented, huge installed base, easy to source spares

Weaknesses

  • More expensive per unit than generic N20/N30 motors
  • Bare motor (not brushless) has lower efficiency and shorter brush life than premium brushless drive pods
  • 600:1 and 300:1 ratios are fragile-ish under sustained stall loads

Notes

Base Mabuchi FK-050 motor spec (same for every ratio): 11,530 RPM no-load and 1.3A stall current, quoted for the 7.4V (2S LiPo) range typical of antweights; FingerTech states it runs 3V-18.5V. Bare-motor stall torque is ~0.0461 kg-cm; output stall torque at the wheel shaft is approximately bare_stall_torque x ratio x gearbox_efficiency, using ~0.75 efficiency for a 2-3 stage spur gearbox (medium confidence, not officially published). Full ratio ladder: 11.1, 22.2, 33.3, 50, 83.3 (discontinued/low stock), 100, 200, 300, 600:1. Weight by ratio: 11:1 = 28g, 22:1 through 100:1 = 29g, 200:1/300:1 = 31g, 600:1 = 32g. Shaft is 3mm with a flat, 38mm standard length, with a shorter 22mm version available on 22:1/33:1/50:1 for tighter builds. 11mm circular face-mount pattern for standard FingerTech motor mounts.
