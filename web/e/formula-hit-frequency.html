Hit frequency from RPM and tooth count

How many times per second a spinning weapon's tooth (or teeth) strike, used both for the bite-depth formula above and for estimating cumulative impact loading on the weapon mount and motor over a fight.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightbiteweapon-geometrytooth-counthit-frequency

Expression

f_hit = (rpm / 60) * tooth_count

Variables

f_hithit frequency, hits/s
rpmweapon rotational speed, rpm
tooth_countnumber of teeth/striking points on the weapon

Worked example

Same weapon re-designed with 2 teeth instead of 1, same rpm = 20,000: f_hit = (20000/60) * 2 = 666.7 hits/s -- double the single-tooth hit rate. At the same v_closing = 1.5 m/s, bite_depth becomes 1.5/666.7 = 0.00225 m (2.25 mm), HALF the single-tooth bite depth -- the direct trade-off between hit rate and per-hit penetration depth that drives the classic 'single big tooth vs multiple smaller teeth' vertical-spinner design debate.
