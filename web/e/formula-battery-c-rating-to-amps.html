Battery C-rating to continuous amps

Converts a LiPo pack's C-rating (a multiple of its own capacity) into an actual continuous-current figure in amps, the number that needs to be compared against total robot current draw.

Formulaantweightplastic-antweightelectricalbatteryc-rating

Expression

I_continuous = C_rating * Capacity_Ah

Variables

I_continuouscontinuous discharge current the pack is rated for, A
C_ratingbattery C-rating, dimensionless multiple
Capacity_Ahbattery capacity, Ah (= mAh / 1000)

Worked example

A 300 mAh (0.3 Ah) 2S LiPo rated at 60C: I_continuous = 60 * 0.3 = 18 A.
