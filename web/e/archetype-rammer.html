Rammer

A reinforced nose and a powerful drivetrain — damage comes from the whole robot's momentum.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightrammerbeginnerbudget

Strengths

  • Simple and durable
  • All the weight goes into drive and structure
  • Can genuinely damage lightly built opponents

Weaknesses

  • A 454 g robot carries very little momentum — ramming damage is modest
  • Loses the wedge war to anything with a proper edge
  • Every impact is taken by your own structure too

Notes

Ramming works better in heavier classes where kinetic energy scales with mass. At 1 lb the energy in a full-speed ram is small next to even a modest spinner. Ram robots at antweight are better understood as control bots with a hard front.

  • Control Bot

Weak against

  • Drum Spinner

  • Undercutter
