Plastic Ant Horizontal (Bar) Spinner

A single spinning bar or blade on a vertical axis (spinning in the horizontal plane, striking outward in a circle), the dominant weapon type in metal antweight but a comparatively weak and fragile choice in plastic because the weapon tip itself must be printed plastic rather than hardened steel.

Archetypeplastic-antweightspinnerhorizontal-spinnerbar-spinnermetaweak-in-plastic

Strengths

  • Can hit from any side without needing to line up a face, useful defensively
  • Simple single-part weapon geometry is easy to print and swap

Weaknesses

  • A thin printed bar tip is the weakest possible cross-section in the weakest material allowed in the class — it is the archetype most likely to shatter on its own first hit
  • Raises the robot's center of gravity and exposes a large flat profile for wedges/lifters to exploit
  • Kinetic energy transfer to the opponent is poor compared to metal because the bar has to be kept thick (hence slower or lighter) to survive its own swing

Notes

This is the archetype most changed by the plastic-only rule. In metal antweight, bar spinners with hardened steel or titanium tips are a top-tier weapon; in plastic, the same geometry with a printed tip is fragile and prone to catastrophic self-destruction, which is a large part of why drums and vertical spinners with thick printed hubs dominate the plastic meta instead.
