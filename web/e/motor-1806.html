1806 brushless motor (weapon)

18mm x 6mm stator motor from the older 200-250 class mini-quad era; a long-running staple antweight weapon motor thanks to its 3mm shaft and 16x16mm M3 mount that make it easy to bolt straight to a hub or pulley.

Componentantweightweapon-motorsize-ladderoutrunnerbread-and-butter

Strengths

  • 3mm shaft is much stiffer than 1.5mm 13xx/14xx shafts, tolerates direct-drive hubs better
  • 16x16mm M3 mount matches many printed and metal mount patterns
  • Wide KV selection (1400-2700kv) still sold new

Weaknesses

  • Heavier than 13xx/14xx for builders on a tight plastic-ant budget
  • Still uses drone-grade sleeve/ball bearings, not a combat-rated shaft
