Wedge

A low inclined plane whose job is to get beneath an opponent and take away their traction. The foundation of control robotics.

Archetypeantweightplastic-antweightcontrolpassivebeginnermeta

Strengths

  • No weapon motor means the entire weight budget goes to drive and armour
  • Beats every archetype whose weapon cannot reach below its own edge
  • Extremely reliable — there is almost nothing to break
  • Cheapest competitive robot to build

Weaknesses

  • Cannot generate damage, so it must win on control and judges' decisions
  • Loses to anything that attacks below the wedge line
  • A wedge that gets high-sided is helpless

Notes

A wedge wins by making the opponent's weapon irrelevant. Ground clearance and edge quality matter more than anything else: the leading edge must be thin, hard, flat against the floor, and backed so it does not curl. A wedge that lifts even two millimetres off the floor stops being a wedge.
