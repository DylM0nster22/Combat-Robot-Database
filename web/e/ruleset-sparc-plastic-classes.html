SPARC Plastic Classes (Robot Construction Specifications v1.4, §4.4)

The template ruleset most North American plastic-ant events build from. Defines the class's spirit, the exact allowed plastics list, and the non-plastic component exemption in a single short section that individual events then adopt with a 'deviations' addendum.

Notes

This is the single most copy-pasted plastic-ant ruleset in the US; SCAR, Robot Ruckus and many one-off regional events either quote it directly or diverge from it in a clearly marked 'deviations' section per SPARC's own template instructions.
