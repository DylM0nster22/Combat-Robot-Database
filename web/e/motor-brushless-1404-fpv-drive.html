1404-size Brushless Motor (FPV-derived) for Custom Drive Pods

A 14mm-diameter, 4mm-tall outrunner brushless motor, larger-stator sibling of the 1104/1106 sizes, used in higher-power antweight or lighter beetleweight custom drive builds where more torque/current handling is wanted from a similarly slim motor.

Strengths

  • More stator volume than 1104/1106 for a given height, so more torque/current capability
  • Same FPV-market kv/winding flexibility

Weaknesses

  • Larger diameter can be harder to fit inside a slim antweight chassis
  • Same brushless-drive complexity: reversing ESC required, sensorless cogging risk at low speed

dia mm14
length mm4

Notes

Used mostly in custom-built drive pods (3D-printed or machined gearbox housing) rather than sold as a complete antweight-ready gearmotor product; treat as a component a builder pairs with their own gear reduction.
