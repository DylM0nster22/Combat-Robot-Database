Fairyweight (150g)

The smallest weight class in common use, capped at 150 grams (about 0.33 lb). In the US/SPARC world it is called Fairyweight; confusingly, this exact 150g limit is what British builders call 'Antweight', which is far lighter than the US 1lb antweight. It is a popular entry point because parts are cheap and damage is minimal.

Notes

Robots must also fit within a 150mm x 150mm footprint under UK/AWS rules, with no overhang at any height. Non-rotary/non-rolling locomotion (walkers, shufflers) gets a bonus up to 225g under UK rules (see plastic-antweight and walker-bonus term entries).
