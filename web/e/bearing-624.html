624 bearing

Deep-groove ball bearing, 4 mm bore x 13 mm OD x 5 mm wide.

Componentantweightplastic-antweight

Strengths

  • Cheap and available everywhere
  • Standard metric size, easy to design around

Weaknesses

  • Shielded versions trap debris; open versions let it in
  • Will not survive an unbalanced weapon for long

Specifications

bore mm4
od mm13
width mm5

Notes

4 mm bore. A good step up when a 3 mm shaft is bending. Buy shielded (ZZ) for combat: open bearings fill with debris and rubber-sealed ones add drag. Expect bearings to be consumables on any weapon that takes hits.
