1018 Mild Steel

Low-carbon general-purpose steel — cheap, tough, easy to weld and machine, used for non-critical structural parts, brackets, and low-budget armor where weight isn't the top priority.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightmild-steelstructurebudget

Strengths

  • Very cheap
  • Easy to weld/machine/work with basic hobby tools
  • Tough, ductile, dents rather than shatters

Weaknesses

  • Heavy — density similar to hardened steels without the hardness benefit
  • Rarely fits a 1 lb weight budget as primary armor

Notes

1018 is mostly a practice/prototyping material in antweights — great for a first "beater" bot chassis or non-critical brackets where you don't want to risk expensive titanium or tool steel, but too heavy to be competitive as final armor at 1 lb.
