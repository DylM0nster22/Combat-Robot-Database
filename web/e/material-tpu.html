TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane)

A rubber-like flexible printable filament popular in the wider combat-robot community for flexible armor panels and wheel tires because it bends and springs back instead of cracking — but it is NOT on the standard SPARC/SCAR approved-material list and is explicitly banned by name in SCAR's FAQ, making it a key org-to-org variance point in the plastic-ant class.

Materialplastic-antweightfilamentflexibleorg-variancecontroversial

Strengths

  • Excellent energy absorption; deflects and springs back rather than cracking
  • Great for wheel tires and shock-mounts when the local ruleset allows it

Weaknesses

  • NOT on the base SPARC 4.4.1 list (PET/PETG/ABS/PLA/PLA+ only) unless an organizer grants an explicit exception
  • Explicitly named as banned by SCAR's Plastic Ant FAQ, including for wheel hubs

Notes

Builders should never assume TPU is legal in a plastic-ant event just because it's a 3D-printed plastic — check the specific event's material list or FAQ before designing it in.
