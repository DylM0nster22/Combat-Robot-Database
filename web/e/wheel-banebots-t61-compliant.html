Banebots T61 Compliant Wheel

A compliant (flexible tread) wheel line from Banebots that conforms to surfaces for extra grip and control, sold in 6 hardness options, 4 diameters, and 4 hub-shaft sizes, primarily sized for beetleweight/hobbyweight but with small diameters usable in heavier antweight or control-bot-focused builds; the smaller Banebots T40 line is aimed more directly at ant/fairy scale.

Componentantweightbeetleweightwheelbanebotscomplianttraction

Strengths

  • Compliant rubber tread conforms to the arena floor for better grip and control than a rigid wheel
  • Wide range of hardness options (10A soft/max-grip up to 60A firm/durable) to tune grip vs longevity
  • Well-known, widely trusted brand in combat robotics with a long track record

Weaknesses

  • Heavier than a foam or TPU wheel of similar diameter — a real cost on a 1 lb weight budget
  • Smallest available diameters may still be larger/heavier than what a competitive lightweight antweight wants

Notes

T61 compliant hardness options: 10A (yellow, maximum flexibility/grip) and 60A (black, firmer/more durable) were specifically confirmed; the line is stated to offer 6 hardness options total, 4 diameters, and 4 hub shaft sizes. The smaller T40 line is described as better suited to antweight/fairyweight builds.
