5 mm weapon shaft

A stiff weapon shaft for higher-energy antweight weapons, usually hardened steel or drill rod.

Componentantweightplastic-antweight

Strengths

  • Stiffness scales with the fourth power of diameter, so 5 mm is dramatically stiffer than 3 mm
  • Room to machine a proper flat or broach a hex

Weaknesses

  • Heavier
  • Needs larger bearings, which means a bigger hub

Specifications

dia mm5
typical materialhardened steel, drill rod, or Ti

Notes

Bending stiffness goes as d^4, so going from 3 mm to 5 mm is roughly a 7.7x increase in stiffness for about 2.8x the mass. On any weapon doing real damage this is one of the best weight-for-reliability trades available.
