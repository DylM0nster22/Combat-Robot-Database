Judging Criteria

When a match goes the distance, judges score it — usually on damage, aggression and control.

Termantweightplastic-antweightstrategy

Notes

Most rulesets weight damage most heavily, then aggression, then control, though the exact split varies by organisation. Practical consequences: a robot that lands two clean hits and then hides often beats one that pushed for three minutes without damaging anything; conversely a control robot that pins, carries and dominates position can beat a spinner that missed all match. Know your event's published split before you plan a strategy around it.
