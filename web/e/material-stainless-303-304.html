Stainless Steel 303/304

Austenitic stainless steel used mainly for fasteners, shafts, and small hardware in combat robots rather than armor, valued for corrosion resistance and easy sourcing as standard hardware.

Materialantweightplastic-antweightstainlessfastenershardware

Strengths

  • Corrosion resistant — good for shafts/hardware exposed to arena debris
  • Standard hardware sizes readily available

Weaknesses

  • Not hardenable by heat treatment (austenitic) so not used for wear/weapon parts
  • Heavier and weaker than alloy steel for structural use

Notes

Stainless shows up in antweights almost entirely as socket-head screws, shoulder bolts, and off-the-shelf shafting rather than as an engineered armor or weapon material.
