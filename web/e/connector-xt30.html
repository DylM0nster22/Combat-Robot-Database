XT30 Connector

A compact bullet-style connector rated for roughly 15-30A continuous depending on source and wire gauge, the standard 'step up' connector from tiny JST leads once an antweight's 3S or 4S pack needs to support real weapon current.

Notes

XT30 is used on most of the CNHL and GNB antweight-size packs referenced in this file; it is a reasonable middle ground for antweight current levels — small enough to fit the chassis, rated well above realistic sustained antweight draw (see the power-budget chunk), while leaving XT60/XT90 for larger weight classes.
