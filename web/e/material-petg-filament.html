PETG Filament

Glycol-modified PET filament — tougher and more layer-adhesion-forgiving than PLA with better temperature resistance, at the cost of being stringier/harder to print cleanly and somewhat less stiff.

Materialantweightplastic-antweight3d-printedtoughmoderate-difficulty

Strengths

  • More impact-tough/ductile than PLA — bends/deforms more before failing
  • Better layer adhesion under stress than ABS
  • Higher heat resistance than PLA (~75°C HDT)
  • Good chemical/moisture resistance

Weaknesses

  • Lower stiffness than PLA — parts flex more under load, which can be bad for precision-fit mechanisms
  • Stringier and gummier to print cleanly, especially at high speed
  • Can be harder to sand/finish than PLA

Notes

PETG is the second most common plastic-antweight print material after PLA, generally chosen for parts that need more give (drivetrain mounts, less-rigid armor flaps) rather than maximum stiffness — it's the toughness/ease-of-printing compromise between brittle-but-stiff PLA and harder-to-print ABS/nylon.
