A 1 lb antweight is 454 g. Where those grams go is the single most consequential set of decisions in the design, and the split is largely determined by archetype.

In grams against 454 g, a 32% weapon share is 145 g and a 20% drive share is 91 g.

Three observations that catch people out:

Electronics is nearly fixed. A receiver, ESCs, wiring and connectors come to something like 50-65 g regardless of archetype. You cannot economise your way out of it, so it is effectively a fixed tax on the budget.

Battery is smaller than people expect. A 450 mAh 3S pack is around 35-40 g. Antweight matches are short; capacity is rarely the constraint, sag is.

Leave 3-5% slack. Robots always come in heavier than the model says — fasteners, wire, glue, threadlocker and the bits you add in the pits. Budgeting to exactly 454 g means failing inspection.
