Weight Bonuses: Walkers, Shufflers, and Multibots Across Organizations

Rules Mechanics

Because a true walking mechanism (linked legs with no continuous rolling or cam contact against the floor) is mechanically heavier and less power-efficient than a wheel for the same ground speed, essentially every major ruleset gives walking robots a weight allowance over the standard class limit, and several give a smaller allowance to 'shufflers' or other non-wheeled-but-not-strictly-walking locomotion.

SPARC: true walkers get a 100% bonus (i.e. double the normal class weight), and shufflers or other qualifying non-wheeled locomotion can get up to a 50% bonus, depending on exactly how the mechanism contacts the floor. A walker is formally defined as having no rolling elements and no continuous rolling or cam-operated motion in contact with the floor, directly or through a linkage — a strict definition meant to stop 'fake walkers' (e.g. a wheel disguised inside a leg-shaped shroud) from claiming the bonus.

NHRL: publishes exact numbers per class. Non-Traditional Locomotion gives +1.5 lb in the 3 lb class, +6 lb in the 12 lb class, and +15 lb in the 30 lb class (a 50% bonus). True Walker status gives +3 lb, +12 lb, and +30 lb respectively in those same classes — a full 100% bonus, meaning a 3 lb-class true walker may legally weigh 6 lb.

RoboGames: runs entirely separate 'walking-bonus classes' that are exactly double the corresponding standard class: 150 g becomes 300 g, 1 lb becomes 2 lb, 3 lb becomes 6 lb, 120 lb becomes 240 lb, and 250 lb becomes 500 lb.

BattleBots: heavyweight is normally 250 lb, but a robot that walks via articulated legs and meets additional design criteria may compete up to 500 lb — again a 100% bonus, matching the pattern seen everywhere else.

AWS (UK): antweight is 150 g for roller-locomotion robots but 225 g for non-roller robots, a 50% bonus rather than 100%, reflecting the UK ruleset's generally tighter tolerances at these very small masses.

Multibot/clusterbot teams (two or more separately-armored robots fighting as one entry) share their class's weight limit across all sub-units, with further bonuses if every sub-unit qualifies as a walker. A worked SPARC-style example: a 12 lb class multibot made of one walker and one shuffler gets an 18 lb combined allowance, while a 12 lb multibot made of two full walkers gets 24 lb. AWS handles mixed roller/non-roller clusterbots with an explicit formula: Roller allowance = Roller class limit − (2/3 × total non-roller weight), which prevents a team from gaming the system by putting almost all of the robot's mass into non-rolling parts to harvest the bonus.
