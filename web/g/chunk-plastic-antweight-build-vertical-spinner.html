This leaves roughly 44g of headroom under the 454g limit, typically used to thicken the weapon guard, add a printed skid plate underneath, or carry a small amount of printed ballast for weight distribution.

Weight budget as percentages: weapon system (disc+shaft+bearings+motor+ESC = 94g) ≈ 23%, drive system (motors+wheels+combo board = 44g) ≈ 11%, armor/structure (chassis+shell = 220g) ≈ 54%, electronics (battery+wiring = 33g) ≈ 8%, fasteners ≈ 3%. The armor/structure share is dramatically higher than a typical metal antweight's, which is the single biggest structural difference the plastic-only rule creates in how weight gets allocated.

Rules-legality notes: the disc, shell and chassis are all printed PETG/PLA+ (approved list); the shaft, bearings, motor, ESC, fasteners, wiring and battery are all covered by the standard motors/electronics/axles/fasteners/adhesives exemption; wheel tire material should be confirmed against the specific event's TPU policy before finalizing.
