Worked run-time sanity check: a 300 mAh (0.3 Ah) pack under an average combined draw of 8 A (mixing idle weapon-maintain current with occasional drive current) gives runtime = Capacity_Ah / I_avg = 0.3/8 = 0.0375 h = 2.25 minutes -- right in the expected range for a spinner-class build, underscoring why battery capacity vs average current draw needs an explicit check rather than an assumption.

Use these ranges as a first filter: if your design's tip speed, KE, or spin-up time falls far outside these bands, re-check your MOI, Kt/Kv, and gear-ratio math before assuming the design itself is simply "better" or "worse" than the norm.
