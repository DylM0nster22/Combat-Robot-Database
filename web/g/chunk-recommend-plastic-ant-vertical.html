Recommended weapon motor: plastic antweight vertical spinner

Application-Specific Recommendations

plastic-antweightrecommendationvertical-spinnerplastic-ant

Plastic antweights run under much tighter total-weight and often lower-cost-per-part budgets than metal 1 lb antweights (many events cap plastic ants around 1 lb / 454g with a plastic-only armor/structure rule, so every gram not spent on the drive/frame can go to the weapon). For a small vertical disc or paddle weapon in the 20-60g range, the 1204-1306-1408 bracket is the sweet spot: light enough (14-30g) to leave the vast majority of the weight budget for chassis and drive, cheap enough (roughly $10-16) to keep several spares on hand, and available in a wide enough KV range (2600-4500kv depending on exact size) to hit a reasonable 150-220 mph tip speed target on a small 2-3 inch disc at 2S-3S.

A builder should size up to a 1408 rather than a 1306 if the weapon disc/paddle is pushing toward the 40-60g range or if the build wants more spin-up torque headroom for a thicker/heavier blade -- the taller 8mm stator of the 1408 gives noticeably more torque than the 6mm-tall 1306 for only a few extra grams. Conversely, a very small (sub-20g) weapon on a genuinely minimal plastic ant can stick with an 1104/1106/1204 to save every possible gram for drive motors and armor thickness, accepting a lower absolute tip speed and KE ceiling in exchange.

Practical KV selection: for a 3 inch (76mm) disc targeting roughly 200 mph tip speed on 3S (11.1V nominal), the required RPM is about 200 * 336.14 / 3 = 22,409 RPM, giving a target KV of roughly 22409/11.1 approx 2020 kv -- squarely in the range of common 1306/1408 windings. Builders should always double-check the exact winding stocked by their chosen vendor rather than assuming a nominal "1306" or "1408" always ships at one fixed KV; these sizes are sold across a wide KV spread (roughly 2200-6000kv) specifically so builders can dial in tip speed without needing a belt reduction.
