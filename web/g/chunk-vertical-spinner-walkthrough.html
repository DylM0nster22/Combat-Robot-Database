Designing a 1 lb vertical spinner from scratch: a numeric walkthrough

Design Walkthroughs

antweightplastic-antweightwalkthroughvertical-spinnerdesign-process

This walkthrough sizes a full metal-weapon vertical spinner for a 454 g (1 lb) antweight, using the formulas on this page end to end.

1. Set targets. Aim for a hard-hitting but achievable tip speed around 150 mph (67 m/s) and a spin-up time under ~1.5 s.

2. Size the weapon. Pick a compound weapon: a 60 g solid disc (radius 28 mm) plus a 10 g bolt-on steel tooth at 30 mm radius, tooth tip reaching out to 32 mm. Using formula-moi-solid-disc and formula-parallel-axis-theorem: I_disc = 0.50.0600.028^2 = 2.35e-5 kgm^2; tooth adds 0.0100.030^2 = 9.0e-6 kg*m^2. **I_total = 3.25e-5 kg*m^2.**

3. Pick the operating speed. Target v_tip = 67 m/s at r_tip = 0.032 m gives omega = v/r = 2094 rad/s = 20,000 rpm (formula-tip-speed rearranged). Stored energy (formula-rotational-ke): KE = 0.5 3.25e-5 2094.4^2 = 71.3 J -- a genuinely hard-hitting 1 lb spinner.

4. Pick the motor. A small brushless weapon motor at Kv = 2750 rpm/V on 3S (11.1 V) gives no-load rpm = 30,525 (formula-no-load-rpm), comfortably above the 20,000 rpm target so the motor isn't being asked to run at its very top end. Using the exact Kt relation (formula-kt-from-kv): Kt = 9.5493/2750 = 0.003472 Nm/A. With a 30 A stall current: T_stall = 0.1042 Nm.

5. Check spin-up time. Average torque across the 0-20,000 rpm range works out to about 0.0701 Nm (formula-spin-up-time), giving t_spinup = Iomega/T_avg = 3.25e-5*2094.4/0.0701 = 0.97 s -- meets the under-1.5s target.

6. Check the gyro-dance risk. At full weapon speed, the tipping-moment threshold for a 90 mm track / 454 g chassis is 0.2004 N*m (formula-tipping-moment-threshold), and the max safe in-place turn rate before the precession torque exceeds that is about 2.94 rad/s (~169 deg/s) (formula-max-safe-turn-rate-gyro) -- driving software/driver technique should respect that limit, or turn in wider arcs.

7. Weight budget. Weapon system (motor+disc+ESC) 30% = 136 g; drive 20% = 91 g; armor/frame 30% = 136 g; electronics 12% = 55 g; fasteners 8% = 36 g (formula-weight-budget-allocation), summing to 454 g.

8. Battery check. A 300 mAh 2S/3S 60C pack gives 18-60A continuous depending on cell count/rating (formula-battery-c-rating-to-amps) -- plenty above the weapon's ~10 A cruise current and enough headroom for the 30 A stall.

Final spec: 3.25e-5 kg*m^2 compound disc weapon, 20,000 rpm / 67 m/s tip speed, 71.3 J stored, ~1.0 s spin-up, 2750 Kv / 30 A-stall brushless weapon motor on 3S, 60A-rated weapon ESC, 90 mm track width chassis for gyro stability, within a 454 g total budget.
