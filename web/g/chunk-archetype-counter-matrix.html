How the archetypes match up

Strategy

antweightplastic-antweightmetastrategymatchup

A compact summary of who beats whom at antweight, and why. Treat these as tendencies, not laws — driving overturns most of them.

AttackerBeatsLoses toWhy
Vertical spinnerWedges, control bots, rammers, hammersHorizontals, undercutters, grabbersUpward damage is unanswerable; but its own weapon and wheels are exposed to horizontal sweeps
DrumWedges, control bots, undercuttersHorizontals, grabbersBest bite of any spinner — it wedges under and then hits upward
Horizontal barVertical spinners, drums, hammersWedges, forkbots, rammersHuge reach and attacks weapons and wheels; passes harmlessly over a good wedge
UndercutterWedges, forkbots, vertical spinnersDrums, grabbersStrikes below the wedge line, so the wedge no longer protects
Wedge / forkbotHorizontals, shells, hammers, rammersVerticals, drums, undercuttersRemoves traction from anything whose weapon cannot reach below its edge
GrabberEvery spinner, once clampedWedges, control botsNeutralises a weapon completely; but must survive the approach
HammerControl bots, invertible bricksAll spinnersConcentrated point damage, but far less total energy than a spinner
ThwackbotControl bots, wedgesAll spinners, grabbersFree weapon, but cannot attack and manoeuvre simultaneously
Flipper (spring)Vertical spinners, wedges, control botsHorizontals, invertible bricksAttacks spinners from underneath where they cannot respond; useless against an invertible opponent

The important structural fact: the wedge beats the horizontal, the vertical beats the wedge, and the horizontal beats the vertical. Because the wedge is cheap and can be bolted onto anything, the stable solution is a vertical spinner carrying wedgelets — which is exactly what the top of the class looks like. The genuine counter-play to that meta is the undercutter, which is why it persists despite being difficult to build.
