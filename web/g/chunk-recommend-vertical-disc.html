Recommended weapon motor: 1 lb vertical disc

Application-Specific Recommendations

antweightrecommendationvertical-spinnerdisc

The 1 lb vertical disc spinner is the single most common antweight archetype, and the 22xx family (2205/2206/2207) direct-driving a 3-4.5 inch steel or hardened-tool-steel disc is the default, extremely well-proven configuration. A 2205 or 2206 at roughly 2000-2400kv on 4S gives a natural direct-drive tip speed in the 200-260 mph range on a 4 inch disc without needing any belt reduction, which keeps the build mechanically simple (motor bolts to frame, disc bolts to motor bell or a hub on the shaft).

The 2207 is worth the small weight premium over a 2205/2206 whenever the disc is on the heavier end (steel discs pushing 100-180g) because its taller stator gives meaningfully more stall torque and thermal margin for the same rough KV, which directly shortens spin-up time and improves survivability against repeated hard stalls. Purpose-built combat hubmotors on the 2207 stator (such as the Repeat 2207 Battle Ready Hubmotor) go a step further by combining the motor and the weapon-mounting hub into one part with a hardened bell and screw-retained mounting interface, removing the classic bell-separation risk of a stock drone 2207 while keeping the same simple bolt-disc-to-bell assembly.

Selection checklist for a 1 lb vertical disc: (1) pick disc diameter and target mass first (most competitive antweight discs run 3.5-4.5 inches, 80-180g); (2) use the tip-speed and required-KV formulas to find the KV that hits 200-250 mph at your intended pack voltage (almost always 4S for this class); (3) choose a 2205-2207 stator in that KV range, favoring the taller (2207) end of that range if the disc is heavy or you want faster spin-up; (4) budget for a hardened or 4-5mm shaft and threadlocked/screw-retained bell rather than trusting a stock drone motor's C-clip, since a vertical disc concentrates enormous shock load directly on the shaft-bell joint on every hit.
